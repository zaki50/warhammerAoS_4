**増援不可**

**ステータス:**

| Move | Health | Save | Control | Ward |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| - | 6 | 4+ | 8+ | 6+ |

**アビリティ:**

- **Siphon Souls**（Passive）
  - 効果: Each time a model from a friendly or enemy unit is slain within 12" of this **MANIFESTATION,** give this **MANIFESTATION** a **soul point,** to a maximum of 6.
- **Soul Eruption**（End of Any Turn）
  - 宣言: If this **MANIFESTATION** has 6 **soul points,** pick any number of enemy units within 6" of this **MANIFESTATION** to be the targets.
  - 効果: Roll 6 dice for each target. For each 4+, inflict 1 mortal damage on the target. Then, reset this **MANIFESTATION**'s **soul points** to 0.

**キーワード:** Manifestation, Endless Spell, Ward (6+), Death, Nighthaunt
