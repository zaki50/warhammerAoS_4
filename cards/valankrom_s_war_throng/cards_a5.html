Battle Damaged：Passive

効果：While this unit has 10 or more damage points, the Attacks characteristic of Karak War Council’s Armoury is 4.

Defend The Throne!：Once Per Turn (Army), Start of Any Turn

宣言：Pick another visible friendly KHAZALITE HOLDS unit wholly within 12" of this unit to be a target. For each grudge mark this unit has, you can pick another eligible unit to be an additional target.

効果：For the rest of the turn, the targets’ melee weapons have Anti-charge (+1 Rend) while they are wholly within 6" of this unit. Add 3" to this range for each grudge mark this unit has.

Karak War Council：Once Per Turn (Army), Your Hero Phase

効果：Pick 1 of the following effects to apply until the start of your next turn:
Stonesmith’s Passage: *The Stonesmith shifts rock and earth to aid the manoeuvres of nearby duardin.*
Add 2" to the Move characteristic of visible friendly KHAZALITE HOLDS INFANTRY units while they are wholly within 12" of this unit. In addition, no mortal damage is inflicted on those friendly units by RETREAT abilities.
Grudgebearer’s Accusation: *The grudgebearer singles out an enemy champion to be the target of the duardin’s vengeance.*
Pick a visible enemy HERO within 18" of this unit to be the target. Friendly KHAZALITE HOLDS INFANTRY units can ignore the effects of the ‘Guarded Hero’ ability on the target when picking targets for their shooting attacks.
Holdseeker’s Protection: *Awakening the runes on their staff, the holdseeker imbues nearby duardin with an aura that repels the attacks of the foe.*
Pick a visible friendly KHAZALITE HOLDS unit wholly within 12" of this unit to be the target. Roll a dice. On a 3+, other than the Companion weapon ability, weapon abilities for attacks that target that friendly unit have no effect.

Forge A Path：Once Per Turn (Army), Reaction: You declared a **MOVE** ability for this unit in your movement phase

効果：Pick a visible friendly KROMDRON INFANTRY unit that is wholly within 6" of this unit, is not in combat and has not been set up this turn to be the target.
Remove the target from the battlefield. After this unit ends its move, you must set up the target wholly within 6" of this unit and more than 9" from all enemy units.

Tectonic Impact：Once Per Turn (Army), Any Charge Phase

宣言：If this unit charged this turn, pick a visible enemy unit within 1" of it to be the target.

効果：Roll a dice. On a 3+, inflict an amount of mortal damage on the target equal to the roll.
