### King Brodd's Stomp Battle Traits（出典: Army of Renown: King Brodd's Stomp）

- **Smash It All To Bits**（Once Per Turn (Army), Any Shooting Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **MEGA-GARGANT** that has not used a **RAMPAGE** ability this turn to use this ability, then pick a terrain feature within its combat range. Then, pick a visible enemy unit within 18" of that **MEGA-GARGANT** to be the target.
  - 効果: Roll a D3 for each friendly and enemy unit on the terrain feature that was picked. On a 2+, inflict an amount of mortal damage on that unit equal to the roll. After rolling for all those units, roll a dice. On a 3+, inflict an amount of mortal damage on the target equal to the roll.
- **Timberrrrr!**（Passive）
  - 効果: Each time a friendly **MEGA‐GARGANT** or **GARGANT** model is slain, before removing it from play, the players must roll off. The winner picks a point on the battlefield within 3" of that model. Inflict D3 mortal damage on each unit (friendly and enemy) within 3" of that point that has a lower Health characteristic than that model.
- **Double Stomp**（Once Per Turn (Army), Any Combat Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **MEGA‑GARGANT** that has not used a **RAMPAGE** ability this turn to use this ability.
  - 効果: Roll a D6. On a 3+, for the rest of the turn, add the result to the Attacks characteristic of the **Almighty Stomp** or **Almightier Stomp** of the unit using this ability.
- **Wrath of Brodd**（Once Per Battle (Army), Your Charge Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **MEGA‑GARGANT** that has not used a **RAMPAGE** ability this turn to use this ability.
  - 効果: If the unit using this ability charged this turn, roll a dice. On a 3+, that unit can move 3D6" but must end that move in combat.
- **Watch This!**（Once Per Turn (Army), Any Combat Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **MEGA‑GARGANT** that has not used a **RAMPAGE** ability this turn to use this ability, then pick an enemy unit in combat with it to be the target.
  - 効果: If the target is a **HERO**, **BEAST**, **MONSTER** or **WAR MACHINE**, roll 6 dice. Otherwise, roll a dice for each model in the target unit. For each roll that is equal to or greater than the target’s Save characteristic, inflict 1 mortal damage on the target. The **MEGA‑GARGANT** using this **RAMPAGE** has **STRIKE‑LAST** for the rest of the turn.
- **Crafty Creepers**（Once Per Turn (Army), Any Combat Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **MEGA‑GARGANT** that has not used a **RAMPAGE** ability this turn to use this ability. Then, pick an enemy **HERO** that has an artefact of power and is in combat with that **MEGA‑GARGANT** to be the target.
  - 効果: Roll a dice. On a 3+, the target no longer has that artefact of power.
- **The World Titan's Prophet**（Passive）
  - 効果: While any other friendly **BRODD’S STOMP** units are within a friendly **King Brodd**’s combat range: • That friendly **King Brodd** has **WARD (5+)**. • Each time you make a successful ward roll for that friendly **King Brodd**, allocate 1 damage point to another friendly **BRODD’S STOMP** unit within its combat range after the damage sequence for that friendly **King Brodd** has been resolved (ward rolls cannot be made for those damage points).

### King Brodd's Stomp Heroic Trait（出典: Army of Renown: King Brodd's Stomp）

**HERO** only

- **I Can Do That Better**（Once Per Battle (Army), Reaction: You declared the ‘Wrath of Brodd’ ability for a friendly unit）
  - 効果: This unit can use the ‘Wrath of Brodd’ ability immediately after the ‘Wrath of Brodd’ ability used by the other friendly unit has been resolved. This unit can do so even if it has used another **RAMPAGE** ability this turn.
