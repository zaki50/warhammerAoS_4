### King Brodd's Stomp Battle Traits（出典: Army of Renown: King Brodd's Stomp）

- **Timberrrrr!**（Passive）
  - 効果: Each time a friendly **BIG** unit or **LITTLE** unit model is slain, before removing it from play, the players must roll off. The winner picks a point on the battlefield within 3" of that model. Inflict D3 mortal damage on each unit (friendly and enemy) within 3" of that point that has a lower Health characteristic than that model.
- **The World Titan's Prophet**（Passive）
  - 効果: While any other friendly **BRODD’S STOMP** units are within a friendly **King Brodd**’s combat range: • That friendly **King Brodd** has **WARD (5+)**. • Each time you make a successful ward roll for that friendly **King Brodd**, allocate 1 damage point to another friendly **BRODD’S STOMP** unit within its combat range after the damage sequence for that friendly **King Brodd** has been resolved (ward rolls cannot be made for those damage points).
- **Wrath of Brodd**（Once Per Battle (Army), Your Charge Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **BIG** unit that has not used a **DESTRUCTIVE IMPULSE** ability this turn to use this ability.
  - 効果: If the unit using this ability charged this turn, roll a dice. On a 3+, that unit can move 3D6" but must end that move in combat. When it does so, it can pass through terrain features and enemy models as if it had **FLY**.’
- **Double Stomp**（Once Per Turn (Army), Any Combat Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **BIG** unit that has not used a **DESTRUCTVE IMPULSE** ability this turn to use this ability.
  - 効果: Roll a D6. On a 3+, for the rest of the turn, add the result to the Attacks characteristic of the **Almighty Stomp** of the unit using this ability.
- **Crafty Creepers**（Once Per Turn (Army), Any Combat Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **BIG** unit that has not used a **DESTRUCTIVE IMPULSE** ability this turn to use this ability. Then, pick an enemy **HERO** that has an artefact of power and is in combat with that **BIG** unit to be the target.
  - 効果: Roll a dice. On a 3+, the target no longer has that artefact of power.
- **Smash It All To Bits**（Once Per Turn (Army), Any Shooting Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **BIG** unit that has not used a **DESTRUCTIVE IMPULSE** ability this turn to use this ability, then pick a terrain feature within its combat range. Then, pick a visible enemy unit within 18" of that **BIG** unit to be the target.
  - 効果: Roll a D3 for each friendly and enemy unit on the terrain feature that was picked. On a 2+, inflict an amount of mortal damage on that unit equal to the roll. After rolling for all those units, roll a dice. On a 3+, inflict an amount of mortal damage on the target equal to the roll.
- **Watch This!**（Once Per Turn (Army), Any Combat Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **BIG** unit that has not used a **DESTRUCTIVE IMPULSE** ability this turn to use this ability, then pick an enemy unit in combat with it to be the target.
  - 効果: If the target is a **HERO**, **BEAST**, **MONSTER** or **WAR MACHINE**, roll 6 dice. Otherwise, roll a dice for each model in the target unit. For each roll that is equal to or greater than the target’s Save characteristic, inflict 1 mortal damage on the target. The **BIG** unit using this **DESTRUCTIVE IMPULSE** ability has **STRIKE‑LAST** for the rest of the turn.
