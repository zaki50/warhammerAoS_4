## バトル特性


### The Iron March Battle Traits（出典: Army of Renown: The Iron March）

- **Form Up, Iron March!**（Start of Battle Round）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **IRON MARCH** unit to use this ability, then pick either 1 or 2 other visible friendly **IRON MARCH** units wholly within 12" of it to be the targets. The unit using this ability and the targets: • cannot be **under orders**. • must each be a different unit type.
  - 効果: For the rest of the battle round, the unit using this ability and the targets are **under orders**. **Designer’s Note:** *‘Unit type’ refers to the name of the warscroll (Core Rules, 4.0), e.g. ‘Cannonade Cogfort’ or ‘Mallus Forgepriest’.*

## その他の強化


### Commanders of the Iron March（出典: Army of Renown: The Iron March）

- **Castellan-Captain Vyan Philsin**（Passive）
  - 効果: Add 1 to wound rolls for combat attacks made by friendly **IRON MARCH INFANTRY** units while they are wholly within 12" of this unit and **under orders**.
- **Commodore De Graçon**（Deployment Phase）
  - 効果: This unit and up to 1 other friendly **IRON MARCH WAR MACHINE** within this unit’s combat range can use the ‘Normal Move’ ability as if it were your movement phase.
- **Castellan-Captain Regan Vandercloud**（Any Charge Phase）
  - 宣言: If this unit charged this turn, pick a visible enemy unit within 1" of it to be the target.
  - 効果: Roll a D3. On a 2+: • Inflict an amount of mortal damage on the target equal to the roll. • The target has a maximum control score of 1 for the rest of the turn.

### Engineers of the Iron March（出典: Army of Renown: The Iron March）

- **Engineer Kirsta Loudren**（Passive）
  - 効果: If this unit is destroyed, before removing it from play, roll a dice for each visible unit (friendly and enemy) within its combat range. On a 3+, inflict an amount of mortal damage on that unit equal to the roll.
- **Engineer Gurrn Ashbrow**（Passive）
  - 効果: Each time this unit uses the ‘Rally’ command, you can make 3 additional rally rolls of D6.
- **Engineer Bartel Holst**（Passive）
  - 効果: This unit has **WARD (6+)** and **STRIKE-LAST**.

### Iron March Auxiliaries（出典: Army of Renown: The Iron March）

- **Battle-Priest Solantra Whyl**（Passive）
  - 効果: This unit has **PRIEST (1)**.
- **Broadmoore and Louse**（Passive）
  - 効果: Add 2 to the Attacks characteristic of this unit’s **Crew’s Leadshotters**. In addition, if this unit is destroyed, before removing it from play, roll a dice. On a 3+, you can set up a **Freeguild Marshal and Relic Envoy** wholly within 6" of this unit and not in combat. That unit has the **IRON MARCH** keyword.
- **Master Sapper Gravelock Fiske**（Passive）
  - 効果: This unit’s ranged weapons have **Anti‑FACTION TERRAIN (+1 Rend)** and **Anti-WAR MACHINE (+1 Rend)**.

### The Iron March Prayer Lore

- **The Chant of Iron**（Your Hero Phase / 詠唱/詠誦値 4）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **IRON MARCH PRIEST** to chant this prayer, pick either a visible enemy unit within 18" of them or a visible friendly **IRON MARCH** unit that is wholly within 12" of them and that has not been picked to be the target of this ability this turn to be the target, then make a chanting roll of D6.
  - 効果: If you picked an enemy unit: • If the chanting roll was 8+, you can pick another eligible enemy unit to be a second target. •Subtract 2" from each target’s Move characteristic until the start of your next turn. If you picked a friendly unit, **Heal (D3)** the target and the chanter. If the chanting roll was 8+, **Heal (D3)** each visible friendly **IRON MARCH** unit that is wholly 12" of the chanter and that has not been healed by this ability this turn instead.
