## バトル特性


### Mawseeker Gollop Battle Traits（出典: Army of Renown: Mawseeker Gollop）

- **Prepare the Feast**（Once Per Turn (Army), Start of Any Turn）
  - 効果: If there is no friendly **Feastmaster** on the battlefield, pick a friendly **GOLLOP HERO** to be the **Feastmaster** for the rest of the battle.
- **Ooh, That Looks Tasty...**（Passive）
  - 効果: Each time an enemy unit is destroyed, you gain a number of **raw ingredients**. The number of **raw ingredients** yielded by a destroyed unit is the Health characteristic of that unit multiplied by the number of starting models in that unit. **Designer’s Note:** *If a destroyed enemy unit had a Health characteristic of 2 and its starting size was 10 models, it would yield 20 raw ingredients when it was destroyed.*
- **Gland Goulash**（Once Per Turn (Army), Any Hero Phase）
  - 宣言: Spend 10 **raw ingredients**.
  - 効果: For the rest of the phase, add 1 to the number of dice rolled, to a maximum of 3, when making casting rolls and unbinding rolls for friendly **GOLLOP** units while they are wholly within 12" of and visible to a friendly **Feastmaster**.
- **Bone-Slurry Soup**（Once Per Turn (Army), Any Shooting Phase）
  - 宣言: Spend 5 **raw ingredients**.
  - 効果: **HEAL (D6)** each friendly **GOLLOP** unit that is wholly within 12" of and visible to a friendly **Feastmaster**.
- **The Organ Platter**（Once Per Turn (Army), Any Combat Phase）
  - 宣言: Spend 20 **raw ingredients**.
  - 効果: For the rest of the phase, while they are wholly within 12" of a friendly **Feastmaster**, melee weapons used by friendly **GOLLOP** units have **Crit (Mortal)**.
- **Gore-Offal Dumplings**（Once Per Turn (Army), End of Any Turn）
  - 宣言: Spend 10 **raw ingredients**.
  - 効果: Each friendly **GOLLOP** unit that is wholly within 12" of and visible to a friendly **Feastmaster** can immediately use a **CHARGE** ability as if it were your charge phase, but subtract 1 from the number of dice rolled when making the charge roll for that unit.
- **Unwashed Innards**（Once Per Turn (Army), Any Movement Phase）
  - 宣言: Spend 5 **raw ingredients**.
  - 効果: For the rest of the phase, add 4 to run rolls for friendly **GOLLOP** units while they are wholly within 12" of and visible to a friendly **Feastmaster**.
- **Fried Limbs**（Once Per Turn (Army), Any Charge Phase）
  - 宣言: Spend 10 **raw ingredients**.
  - 効果: For the rest of the phase, add 3 to charge rolls for friendly **GOLLOP** units while they are wholly within 12" of and visible to a friendly **Feastmaster**.
