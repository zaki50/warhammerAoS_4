## バトル特性


### Beastclaw Alfrostun Battle Traits（出典: Army of Renown: Beastclaw Alfrostun）

- **Masked by the Endless White**（Passive）
  - 効果: Friendly **ALFROSTUN** units are not visible to enemy units more than 12" away.
- **A Clan of Hunters**（Once Per Battle (Army), Deployment Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **Hunters with Sabrefangs** unit to be the target.
  - 効果: The target can use the ‘Through the Frosts’ ability even if another friendly **Hunters with Sabrefangs** unit has used that ability this battle.
- **Grasp of the Everwinter**（Once Per Turn (Army), Your Hero Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick each enemy unit in combat with any friendly **ALFROSTUN** units to be the targets.
  - 効果: Roll a D3 for each target. On a 2+, inflict an amount of mortal damage on the target equal to the roll.
- **Run 'Em Down**（Passive）
  - 効果: Each time an enemy unit in combat with a friendly **ALFROSTUN** unit uses a **RETREAT** ability, after that ability has been resolved, inflict D3 mortal damage on that enemy unit. **Designer’s Note:** *This mortal damage is in addition to any mortal damage inflicted on that enemy unit by that* **RETREAT** *ability.*
- **Fearsome Breed**（Passive）
  - 効果: Subtract 1 from charge rolls for enemy units while they are within 12" of a friendly **ALFROSTUN MONSTER**. Subtract 2 from charge rolls for enemy units while they are within 6" of a friendly **ALFROSTUN MONSTER** instead.

## 英雄特性


### Beastclaw Alfrostun Heroic Trait（出典: Army of Renown: Beastclaw Alfrostun）

(**HERO** only)

- **Hard-Bitten Chieftain**（Passive）
  - 効果: Other than the **Companion** ability, weapon abilities for attacks that target this unit made by enemy units that charged in the same turn have no effect.

## ロア（呪文・祈祷・顕現）


### Beastclaw Alfrostun Prayer Lore

- **Keening Gale**（Your Hero Phase / 詠唱/詠誦値 3）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **ALFROSTUN PRIEST** to chant this prayer, pick a visible friendly **ALFROSTUN** unit wholly within 12" of them to be the target, then make a chanting roll of D6.
  - 効果: Until the start of your next turn, add 2 to run rolls for the target. In addition, if the chanting roll was 6+, until the start of your next turn, add 2 to charge rolls for the target.
- **Avalanche Voice**（Your Hero Phase / 詠唱/詠誦値 4）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **ALFROSTUN PRIEST** to chant this prayer, pick a visible enemy unit within 18" of them to be the target, then make a chanting roll of D6.
  - 効果: Until the start of your next turn, each time your opponent declares a command for the target, as a reaction, make a tremor roll of D6 for the target. On a 5+, unless they spend 1 additional command point to use that command, the command has no effect, it still counts as having been used and the command points spent to use it are still lost. In addition, if the chanting roll was 8+, until the start of your next turn, add 2 to tremor rolls for the target.
- **Terrors of the Old Ice**（Your Hero Phase / 詠唱/詠誦値 4）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **ALFROSTUN PRIEST** to chant this prayer, pick an objective or terrain feature wholly within 18" of them to be the target, then make a chanting roll of D6.
  - 効果: Until the start of your next turn, subtract 10 from the control scores of enemy units while they are contesting the target. If the chanting roll was 8+, until the start of your next turn, enemy units have a maximum control score of 1 while they are contesting the target.
