## バトル特性


### Bonesplitterz Battle Traits（出典: Destruction Battletome: Bonesplitterz）

Bonesplitterz armies can use the following abilities:

- **Spring The Trap**（Passive）
  - 効果: Add 1 to wound rolls for friendly **BONESPLITTERZ** units that charged in the same turn.
- **Bonesplitterz Waaagh!**（Once Per Battle (Army), Your Hero Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick each friendly **BONESPLITTERZ WIZARD** and **BONESPLITTERZ PRIEST** to be the targets.
  - 効果: Add 1 to the power level of each target for the rest of the turn. In addition, friendly **BONESPLITTERZ** units have **WARD** (**5+)** for the rest of the turn.
- **Monster Hunters**（Once Per Turn (Army), Any Charge Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **BONESPLITTERZ** unit in combat with an enemy **MONSTER** to be the target.
  - 効果: For the rest of the turn, add 1 to wound rolls for the target’s combat attacks and add 1 to save rolls for the target.
- **Take Da Bait**（Once Per Battle, Deployment Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick up to 3 friendly **BONESPLITTERZ** units to be the targets.
  - 効果: For the rest of the battle, add 1 to the Attacks characteristic of each target’s melee weapons while it is in combat with an enemy unit that charged in the same turn, and so long as the target has not charged during the battle.

## バトルフォーメーション


### Brutal Rukk

- **Tireless Trackers**（Once Per Battle, Deployment Phase）
  - 効果: Half the friendly non-**HERO BONESPLITTERZ INFANTRY** units on the battlefield (rounding up) can immediately use the ‘Normal Move’ ability as if it were your movement phase.

### Kop Rukk

- **Realm-chompers**（Passive）
  - 効果: Ward rolls cannot be made for enemy units while they are within the combat range of any friendly **BONESPLITTERZ HEROES.**

### Kunnin' Rukk

- **Squint-eye Taktiks**（Once Per Turn (Army), Your Shooting Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick an enemy unit on the battlefield to be the target.
  - 効果: For the rest of the turn, hit rolls for shooting attacks made by friendly **Savage Orruk Arrowboys** units that target the target unit score critical hits on unmodified hit rolls of 5+.

### Snaga Rukk

- **We'z Da Beastiest**（Passive）
  - 効果: Friendly **BONESPLITTERZ CAVALRY** units have **Anti-CHARGE (+1 Rend)**.

## 英雄特性


### Da Beast Wivin（出典: Destruction Battletome: Bonesplitterz）

**HERO** only

- **Killa Instinkt**（Passive）
  - 効果: This unit’s melee weapons have **Crit (Mortal)**.
- **'Orrible Leer**（Passive）
  - 効果: Enemy **MONSTERS** cannot use **RAMPAGE** abilities while they are in combat with this unit.
- **One Wiv Da Beast**（Once Per Battle (Army), Any Hero Phase）
  - 効果: The next time this phase that this unit uses a **SPELL** ability, instead of making a casting roll for this unit, you can use a value of 6 for the roll that cannot be modified.

## アーティファクト・オブ・パワー


### Boss Bones and Other Gubbinz（出典: Destruction Battletome: Bonesplitterz）

**HERO** only

- **Glowin' Tattooz**（Passive）
  - 効果: This unit has **WARD (4+)**.
- **Monsta-killa Chompa**（Passive）
  - 効果: This unit has **STRIKE-FIRST** while it is in combat with an enemy **BEAST** or **MONSTER**.
- **Dokk Juice**（Passive）
  - 効果: If this unit would be destroyed, before removing it from play, roll a dice. On a 3+, this unit is not destroyed and any remaining damage points inflicted on it have no effect. Then, **Heal (1)** this unit. This unit cannot use this ability again for the rest of the battle.

## ロア（呪文・祈祷・顕現）


### Lore of the Savage Beast

- **Fists of Gork**（Your Hero Phase / 詠唱/詠誦値 6）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **BONESPLITTERZ WIZARD** to cast this spell, pick a visible enemy unit within 18" of them to be the target, then make a casting roll of 2D6.
  - 効果: Roll a dice for each model in the target unit. For each 5+, inflict 1 mortal damage on the target.
- **Glowy Green Frenzy**（Your Hero Phase / 詠唱/詠誦値 6）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **BONESPLITTERZ WIZARD** to cast this spell, pick a visible friendly unit wholly within 12" of them to be the target, then make a casting roll of 2D6.
  - 効果: Add 1 to the Rend characteristic of the target’s melee weapons until the start of your next turn, including **Companion** weapons.
- **Gorkamorka's War Cry**（Your Hero Phase / 詠唱/詠誦値 6）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **BONESPLITTERZ WIZARD** to cast this spell, pick a visible enemy unit within 18" of them to be the target, then make a casting roll of 2D6.
  - 効果: Until the start of your next turn, subtract 1 from the number of dice rolled when making charge rolls for the target, to a minimum of 1.

### Prayers of the Living Wilds

- **Bashdokk Dance**（Your Hero Phase / 詠唱/詠誦値 3）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **BONESPLITTERZ PRIEST** to chant this prayer, pick a visible friendly **BONESPLITTERZ** unit wholly within 12" of them to be the target, then make a chanting roll of D6.
  - 効果: Add 1 to charge rolls and run rolls for the target for the rest of the turn. If the chanting roll was 7+, in addition, for the rest of the turn, the target can use **CHARGE** abilities even if it used a **RUN** or **RETREAT** ability in the same turn.
- **Grimdokk Dance**（Your Hero Phase / 詠唱/詠誦値 4）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **BONESPLITTERZ PRIEST** to chant this prayer, pick a visible friendly **BONESPLITTERZ** unit wholly within 12" of them to be the target, then make a chanting roll of D6.
  - 効果: **Heal (3)** the target unit or return a number of slain models to it with a combined Health characteristic of up to 3. If the chanting roll for this prayer was 9+, instead, **Heal (6)** the target unit or return a number of slain models to it with a combined Health characteristic of up to 6.
- **Weirdokk Dance**（Your Hero Phase / 詠唱/詠誦値 5）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **BONESPLITTERZ PRIEST** to chant this prayer. Then make a chanting roll of D6.
  - 効果: Subtract 1 from chanting rolls and casting rolls for enemy **PRIESTS** and **WIZARDS** while they are within 18" of the chanter until the start of your next turn. In addition, if the chanting roll was 11+, enemy **PRIESTS** within 18" of the chanter each lose D6 ritual points and enemy **WIZARDS** cannot use **UNBIND** abilities while they are within 18" of the chanter until the start of your next turn.
