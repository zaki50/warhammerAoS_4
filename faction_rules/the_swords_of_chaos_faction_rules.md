## バトル特性


### The Swords of Chaos Battle Traits（出典: Army of Renown: The Swords of Chaos）

- **Demands of the Dark Gods**（Once Per Turn (Army), Start of Battle Round）
  - 効果: Pick 1 of the following effects: ***Demand of Khorne***: For the rest of the battle round, add 1 to the Rend characteristic of melee weapons used by friendly **SWORDS OF CHAOS** units. ***Demand of Tzeentch***: Pick 1 visible enemy unit within 9" of a friendly **SWORDS OF CHAOS** unit that is not in combat and roll a D6. On a 3+, inflict an amount of mortal damage on that enemy unit equal to the roll. ***Demand of Nurgle***: **Heal (D3)** each friendly **SWORDS OF CHAOS** unit. Then pick a friendly **SWORDS OF CHAOS** unit that healed any damage points this turn. Until the start of the next battle round, that unit has **WARD (6+)**. ***Demand of Slaanesh***: Add 2" to the Move characteristic of friendly **SWORDS OF CHAOS** units until the start of the next battle round.
- **Descent From Ruinous Skies**（Your Movement Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **SWORDS OF CHAOS** unit that is **in the Ruinous Skies**.
  - 効果: Set up that unit anywhere on the battlefield more than 9" from all enemy units.
- **Knights of the First Circle**（Deployment Phase）
  - 宣言: If there are more friendly **SWORDS OF CHAOS** units on the battlefield than there are set up in reserve, pick a friendly **SWORDS OF CHAOS** unit that has not been deployed.
  - 効果: Set up that unit in reserve **in the Ruinous Skies**. It has now been deployed.

## その他の強化


### First Circle Titles（出典: Army of Renown: The Swords of Chaos）

You can include up to 3 First Circle Title enhancements in your army. First Circle Titles can only be given to **Varanguard** units. The same unit cannot have more than 1 First Circle Title.

- **The Blackstorm Apostates**（Passive）
  - 効果: Add 3" to this unit’s Move characteristic.
- **Tamers of Haradh's Torment**（Passive）
  - 効果: Add 1 to the Damage characteristic of this unit’s **Companion** weapons.
- **Betrayers of the Anvilking**（Passive）
  - 効果: This unit has **WARD (6+)**.
- **The Hounds Apocalyptus**（Passive）
  - 効果: While this unit is wholly within enemy territory, add 1 to the Attacks characteristic of this unit’s melee weapons.
- **The Grimroot Order**（Passive）
  - 効果: Add 1 to this unit’s Health characteristic.
