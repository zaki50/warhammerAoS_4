## 戦闘特性


### Gorechosen Champions Battle Traits（出典: Army of Renown: Gorechosen Champions）

- **Murder-won Trophies**（Once Per Battle (Army), Deployment Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **GORECHOSEN CHAMPIONS HERO** to be the target.
  - 効果: Give the target 1 artefact of power from the 'Gorechosen Champions' Army of Renown rules. **Designer's Note:** *This artefact of power is given to the **HERO** during the deployment phase, not during army composition.*
- **Brutal Lashmaster**（Passive）
  - 効果: Each time a friendly **Bloodstoker** uses the 'Whipped to Fury' ability, another friendly **GORECHOSEN CHAMPIONS HERO** within its combat range can be picked to be the target of that ability instead of a non-**HERO BLOODBOUND** unit.
- **Legendary Slayers**（Passive）
  - 効果: **Blood-hungry** weapons used by friendly **GORECHOSEN CHAMPIONS** units have **Crit (Mortal)**.
- **The Red God's Eye**（Passive）
  - 効果: Friendly **GORECHOSEN CHAMPIONS** units have **WARD (6+)**.
- **Hate-fuelled Killers**（Passive）
  - 効果: The first time each friendly **GORECHOSEN CHAMPIONS** unit would be destroyed, before removing it from play, roll a dice. On a 5+, that unit is not destroyed and any remaining damage points inflicted on it have no effect. Then, **Heal (1)** that unit.

## 英雄特性


### Gorechosen Champions Heroic Trait（出典: Army of Renown: Gorechosen Champions）

**HERO** only

- **Crowned in Butchery**（Passive）
  - 効果: Add 20 to this unit's control score while it is in combat.

## 神器


### Gorechosen Champions Artefacts of Power（出典: Army of Renown: Gorechosen Champions）

**HERO** only

- **Warmonger's Icon**（Once Per Battle, Any Combat Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick this unit and up to D3 friendly **GORECHOSEN CHAMPIONS** units within this unit's combat range to be the targets.
  - 効果: The targets have **STRIKE-FIRST** for the rest of the turn.
- **The Scarring Blade**（Any Combat Phase）
  - 効果: Allocate D3 damage points to this unit (ward rolls cannot be made for those damage points). For the rest of the turn, add **X** to the Attacks characteristic of melee weapons used by friendly **GORECHOSEN CHAMPIONS** units for the rest of the turn, where **X** is equal to the number of damage points allocated to this unit this turn by this ability. For the rest of the battle, this unit cannot use the ‘Hate-fuelled Killers’ ability and cannot be healed.
- **Bloodmist Skull**（Passive）
  - 効果: This unit has **WARD (3+)** against damage inflicted by shooting attacks.

## 伝承（呪文・奇蹟・顕現）


### Gorechosen Champions Manifestation Lore

- **Summon Wrath-Axe**（Your Hero Phase / 詠唱/詠誦値 4）
  - 宣言: If there is not a friendly **Wrath-axe** on the battlefield, pick a friendly **BLADES OF KHORNE PRIEST** to chant this prayer, then make a chanting roll of D6.
  - 効果: Set up a **Wrath-axe** wholly within 12" of the chanter, visible to them and more than 9" from all enemy units.

### Gorechosen Champions Prayer Lore

- **Cowed and Broken**（Your Hero Phase / 詠唱/詠誦値 4）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **GORECHOSEN CHAMPIONS PRIEST** to chant this prayer, pick a visible enemy unit within 12" of them that is in combat with a friendly unit to be the target, then make a chanting roll of D6.
  - 効果: Roll 8 dice. If the chanting roll was 8+, add 1 to each roll. For each 4+, inflict 1 mortal damage on the target.
- **Eruption of Apoplexy**（Your Hero Phase / 詠唱/詠誦値 4）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **GORECHOSEN CHAMPIONS PRIEST** to chant this prayer, pick a visible enemy unit within 12" of them that is in combat with a friendly unit to be the target, then make a chanting roll of D6.
  - 効果: Inflict D6 mortal damage on the target. If the target is destroyed, before removing the last model in that unit from play, inflict D3 mortal damage on every unit (friendly and enemy) within 6" of that model, or D6 mortal damage instead if the chanting roll was 8+.
- **Skin of Brass**（Your Hero Phase / 詠唱/詠誦値 4）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **GORECHOSEN CHAMPIONS PRIEST** to chant this prayer, pick a visible friendly unit wholly within 12" of them to be the target, then make a chanting roll of D6.
  - 効果: Until the start of your next turn, subtract 1 from the Rend characteristic of weapons used for attacks that target that friendly unit. If the chanting roll was 8+, you can pick another eligible unit to be a second target.
