## 戦闘特性


### Big Waaagh! Battle Traits（出典: Army of Renown: Big Waaagh!）

- **Notorious Bosses**（Once Per Battle, Deployment Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick up to 1 friendly non-**UNIQUE BIG WAAAGH! IRONJAWZ HERO** and up to 1 friendly non‑**UNIQUE BIG WAAAGH! KRULEBOYZ HERO** that do not have a heroic trait to be the targets.
  - 効果: Give each target 1 heroic trait from the Big Waaagh! heroic traits. **Designer’s Note**: *These heroic traits are given to the targets during the deployment phase, not during army composition.*
- **Rally The Warclans**（Once Per Turn, Your Movement Phase）
  - 宣言: If your general is on the battlefield, pick a friendly **BIG WAAAGH!** unit that started the battle with 3 or more models and that has been destroyed to be the target.
  - 効果: Set up a replacement unit with half the number of models from the target unit (rounding up) wholly within 12" of your general and more than 9" from all enemy units.
- **The Power of The Waaagh!**（Once Per Turn, Your Hero Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick up to 1 friendly **BIG WAAAGH! IRONJAWZ** unit and up to 1 friendly **BIG WAAAGH! KRULEBOYZ** unit to be the targets.
  - 効果: The targets have the **POWER OF THE WAAAGH!** keyword for the rest of the battle round.
- **Possessed By The Power Of The Waaagh!**（Passive）
  - 効果: While a friendly unit has the **POWER OF THE WAAAGH!** keyword: • If that unit has the **IRONJAWZ** keyword, add 1 to run rolls and charge rolls for that unit. • If that unit has the **KRULEBOYZ** keyword, that unit’s attacks score critical hits on unmodified hit rolls of 5+.

## 英雄特性


### Big Waaagh! Heroic Traits（出典: Army of Renown: Big Waaagh!）

**HERO** only

- **Get Krumpin'!**（Once Per Battle, Your Hero Phase）
  - 宣言: You can use this ability if this unit has the **POWER OF THE WAAAGH!** keyword and a friendly unit has not used a **BRUTAL KUNNIN’** ability this turn.
  - 効果: For the rest of the battle round, add 1 to wound rolls for combat attacks made by friendly **BIG WAAAGH!** units while they are wholly within 12" of this unit.
- **A Proper Sneak**（Once Per Battle, Your Hero Phase）
  - 宣言: You can use this ability if this unit has the **POWER OF THE WAAAGH!** keyword and a friendly unit has not used a **BRUTAL KUNNIN’** ability this turn.
  - 効果: For the rest of the battle round, friendly **BIG WAAAGH!** units have **WARD (5+)** while they are wholly within 12" of this unit.
- **Takin' Names**（Passive）
  - 効果: If an enemy **HERO** is destroyed by this unit, this unit has the **POWER OF THE WAAAGH!** keyword for the rest of the battle.
- **Da Old One-Two**（Once Per Battle, Your Hero Phase）
  - 宣言: You can use this ability if this unit has the **POWER OF THE WAAAGH!** keyword and a friendly unit has not used a **BRUTAL KUNNIN’** ability this turn.
  - 効果: For the rest of the battle round, friendly **BIG WAAAGH!** units’ melee weapons have **CRIT (2 HITS)** while they are wholly within 12" of this unit.

**HERO** only

  - 効果: Each time you make an unmodified save roll of 6 for a combat attack that targets this unit, inflict 1 mortal damage on the attacking unit after the **FIGHT** ability has been resolved.

## 伝承（呪文・奇蹟・顕現）


### Big Waaagh! Manifestation Lore

- **Summon Foot of Gork**（Your Hero Phase / 詠唱/詠誦値 7）
  - 宣言: If there is not a friendly **Foot of Gork** on the battlefield, pick a friendly **BIG WAAAGH! WIZARD** to cast this spell, then make a casting roll of 2D6.
  - 効果: Set up a **Foot of Gork** wholly within 12" of the caster, visible to them and more than 9" from all enemy units. A **Foot of Gork** has 2 parts that must be set up within 9" of each other.

### Big Waaagh! Prayer Lore

- **Unstoppable Waaagh!-beats**（Your Hero Phase / 詠唱/詠誦値 4）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **BIG WAAAGH! PRIEST** to chant this prayer, pick a visible friendly non-**HERO BIG WAAAGH! INFANTRY** unit that is in combat and wholly within 12" of them to be the target, then make a chanting roll of D6.
  - 効果: The target can use 2 **FIGHT** abilities this turn. After the first is used, however, the target has **STRIKE‑LAST** for the rest of the turn. If the chanting roll was 10+, you can pick another eligible unit to be a second target.

### Big Waaagh! Spell Lore

- **Two Headz As One**（Your Hero Phase / 詠唱/詠誦値 7）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **BIG WAAAGH! WIZARD** to cast this spell, pick a visible friendly **BIG WAAAGH! INFANTRY** unit wholly within 12" of them to be the target, then make a casting roll of 2D6.
  - 効果: If the target is an **IRONJAWZ** unit, until the start of your next turn, add 3 to the target’s control score while it is wholly within 12" of any friendly **KRULEBOYZ** units. If the target is a **KRULEBOYZ** unit, until the start of your next turn, add 2" to the target’s Move characteristic while it is wholly within 12" of any friendly **IRONJAWZ** units.
