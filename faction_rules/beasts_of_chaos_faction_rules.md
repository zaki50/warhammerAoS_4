## バトル特性


### Beasts of Chaos Battle Traits（出典: Chaos Battletome: Beasts of Chaos）

Beasts of Chaos armies can use the following abilities:

- **Rituals of Ruin**（Once Per Turn (Army), Your Hero Phase）
  - 効果: Pick 1 of the following effects: ***Warping Curse:*** Pick an enemy unit within 12" of a friendly **BEASTS OF CHAOS HERO** or, if there is a friendly **BEASTS OF CHAOS HERO** in reserve, within 12" of a battlefield edge to be the target. Then, roll a D3. On a 2+, inflict an amount of mortal damage on the target equal to the roll. ***Brand of Wild Fury:*** Pick a friendly **BEASTS OF CHAOS** unit wholly within 12" of a friendly **BEASTS OF CHAOS HERO** or, if there is a friendly **BEASTS OF CHAOS HERO** in reserve, wholly within 12" of a battlefield edge to be the target. Then, roll a dice. On a 2+, the target has **WARD (6+)** for the rest of the turn. ***Bestial Instinct:*** Pick a friendly **BEASTS OF CHAOS** unit wholly within 12" of a friendly **BEASTS OF CHAOS HERO** or, if there is a friendly **BEASTS OF CHAOS HERO** in reserve, wholly within 12" of a battlefield edge to be the target. Then, roll a dice. On a 2+, add 1 to wound rolls for the target’s combat attacks for the rest of the turn.
- **Beastherd Ambush**（Your Movement Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **BEASTS OF CHAOS** unit that is **in ambush**.
  - 効果: Set up that unit on the battlefield wholly within 9" of a battlefield edge and more than 9" from all enemy units. For the rest of the turn, add 1 to charge rolls for that unit.
- **Masters of the Wilderness**（Deployment Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **BEASTS OF CHAOS** unit that has not been deployed.
  - 効果: Set up that unit in reserve **in ambush**. It has now been deployed. **Designer’s Note:** *Any number of friendly **BEASTS OF CHAOS** units can start the battle in reserve – even your entire Beasts of Chaos army!*

## バトルフォーメーション


### Almighty Beastherd（Legends）

- **Arch-Despoilers**（Passive）
  - 効果: Add 1 to hit rolls for combat attacks made by friendly **BEASTS OF CHAOS** units that target an enemy unit that is contesting an objective you do not control.

### Hungering Warherd（Legends）

- **Bloodgreed**（End of Any Turn）
  - 宣言: Pick each friendly **WARHERD** unit that used a **FIGHT** ability this turn to be the targets.
  - 効果: **Heal (3)** each target.

### Marauding Brayherd（Legends）

- **Fiends of the Wilds**（Once Per Turn (Army), Reaction: You declared a CHARGE ability for a friendly BRAYHERD unit）
  - 効果: Change one of the dice in the charge roll to a 4.

### Thunderscorn Stormherd（Legends）

- **Raging Storm**（Your Hero Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick each friendly **THUNDERSCORN** unit on the battlefield to be the friendly targets, then pick each enemy unit in combat with any friendly **THUNDERSCORN** units to be the enemy targets.
  - 効果: **Heal (1)** each friendly target. Then, roll a D3 for each enemy target, if any. On a 2+, inflict an amount of mortal damage on the enemy target equal to the roll.

## 英雄特性


### Alphabeast Instincts（出典: Chaos Battletome: Beasts of Chaos）

**HERO** only

- **Death-grip**（Once Per Turn, Reaction: Opponent declared a SHOOT ability that targeted a friendly BEASTS OF CHAOS unit）
  - 効果: If that friendly **BEASTS OF CHAOS** unit is not in combat and is wholly within 18" of this unit, after the **SHOOT** ability has been resolved, that friendly unit can move 2D6". It can move into combat.
- **Bestial Cunning**（Your Movement Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick up to 3 friendly **BEASTS OF CHAOS** units wholly within 12" of this unit that were set up this turn using the ‘Beastherd Ambush’ ability to be the targets.
  - 効果: Each target can immediately move D3". The targets cannot move into combat during any part of that move.
- **Propagator of Ruin**（Passive）
  - 効果: While this unit is on the battlefield, you can use the ‘Rituals of Ruin’ ability twice per turn in your hero phase instead of once per turn. You cannot pick the same effect more than once per turn.

## アーティファクト・オブ・パワー


### Anarchic Relics（出典: Chaos Battletome: Beasts of Chaos）

**HERO** only

- **Slitherwrack Helm**（Passive）
  - 効果: Subtract 1 from charge rolls for enemy units while they are within 12" of this unit.
- **Gnarlstaff of Morghur**（Your Hero Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a visible enemy unit within 12" of this unit to be the target.
  - 効果: Roll a dice. On a 2+, ward rolls cannot be made for the target for the rest of the turn.
- **Brayblast Trumpet**（Your Movement Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly non-**UNIQUE BEASTS OF CHAOS INFANTRY** or **CAVALRY** unit that started the battle with 2 or more models and that has been destroyed to be the target.
  - 効果: Roll a dice. On a 4+, set up a replacement unit with half the number of models from the target unit (rounding up) wholly within 12" of a battlefield edge and more than 9" from all enemy units.

  - 効果: Set up a **Ravening Direflock** wholly within 18" of the caster and more than 9" from all enemy units. A **Ravening Direflock** has 3 parts that must each be set up within 1" of at least 1 other part.

  - 宣言: If there is not a friendly **Wildfire Taurus** on the battlefield, pick a friendly **BEASTS OF CHAOS WIZARD** to cast this spell, then make a casting roll of 2D6.

### Lore of the Twisted Wilds

- **Devolve**（Your Hero Phase / 詠唱/詠誦値 7）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **BEASTS OF CHAOS WIZARD** to cast this spell, pick a visible enemy non-**HERO** unit within 12" of them to be the target, then make a casting roll of 2D6.
  - 効果: Until the start of your next turn: • The target unit has a maximum control score of 1. • The target cannot use commands. • Each time the target moves, if the caster is on the battlefield, the target must end that move no further from the caster than it was at the start of the move.
- **Viletide**（Your Hero Phase / 詠唱/詠誦値 7）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **BEASTS OF CHAOS WIZARD** to cast this spell, pick a visible enemy unit within 12" of them to be the target, then make a casting roll of 2D6.
  - 効果: Inflict D3 mortal damage on the target. In addition, until the start of your next turn, the target cannot use commands.
- **Vicious Stranglethorns**（Your Hero Phase / 詠唱/詠誦値 8）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **BEASTS OF CHAOS WIZARD** to cast this spell, pick a visible enemy unit within 12" of them to be the target, then make a casting roll of 2D6.
  - 効果: Until the start of your next turn: • Halve the target’s Move characteristic. • Subtract 1 from the number of dice rolled when making charge rolls for the target, to a minimum of 1.
