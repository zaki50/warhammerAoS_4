## バトル特性


### Cycle of Corruption Battle Traits（出典: Army of Renown: Cycle of Corruption）

- **Revolution and Revulsion**（Once Per Battle Round (Army), Start of Battle Round）
  - 宣言: You must use this ability at the start of each battle round.
  - 効果: If it is the first battle round, roll a dice to determine which **CYCLE** ability is ripe for the rest of the battle round. 1 ‘Unnatural Vitality’ 2 ‘Fecund Vigour’ 3 ‘Burgeoning Filth’ 4 ‘Plague of Misery’ 5 ‘Nauseous Revulsion’ 6 ‘Rampant Disease’ 7 ‘Corrupted Regrowth’ If it is not the first battle round, the next **CYCLE** ability in the sequence is ripe for the rest of the battle round. If the ‘Corrupted Regrowth’ ability was ripe last battle round, the ‘Unnatural Vitality’ ability is ripe this battle round. You can only use a **CYCLE** ability if it is ripe. Only 1 **CYCLE** ability is ripe each battle round.
- **Unnatural Vitality**（Passive）
  - 効果: Friendly non-**MONSTER CYCLE OF CORRUPTION HEROES** have **WARD (4+)**.
- **Fecund Vigour**（Once Per Turn (Army), Your Hero Phase）
  - 効果: **HEAL (D3)** each friendly **CYCLE OF CORRUPTION** unit.
- **Burgeoning Filth**（Passive）
  - 効果: Subtract 3 from the control scores of enemy units while they are in combat with any friendly **CYCLE OF CORRUPTION** units.
- **Plague of Misery**（Passive）
  - 効果: While enemy units are within 12" of any friendly **CYCLE OF CORRUPTION** units, they cannot use abilities that heal or return slain models to a unit.
- **Nauseous Revulsion**（Passive）
  - 効果: Subtract 1 from charge rolls for enemy units.
- **Rampant Disease**（Passive）
  - 効果: Melee weapons used by friendly non-**HERO CYCLE OF CORRUPTION** units have **Crit (2 Hits)**.
- **Corrupted Regrowth**（Once Per Turn (Army), Your Hero Phase）
  - 効果: **Heal (7)** each friendly **Feculent Gnarlmaw**. If there are no friendly **Feculent Gnarlmaws** on the battlefield, you can set up a **Feculent Gnarlmaw** on the battlefield more than 9" from all enemy units and more than 3" from all objectives and other terrain features.

### Cycle of Corruption Prayer Lore

- **Bacterial Blessing**（Your Hero Phase / 詠唱/詠誦値 4）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **CYCLE OF CORRUPTION PRIEST** to chant this prayer, pick a visible friendly **CYCLE OF CORRUPTION** unit wholly within 12" of them to be the target, then make a chanting roll of D6.
  - 効果: Until the start of your next turn, add 1 to wound rolls for the target’s combat attacks. If the chanting roll was 7+, you can pick another eligible unit to be a second target.
- **Virulent Agonies**（Your Hero Phase / 詠唱/詠誦値 4）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **CYCLE OF CORRUPTION PRIEST** to chant this prayer, pick a visible enemy unit within 18" of them to be the target, then make a chanting roll of D6.
  - 効果: Inflict 1 mortal damage on the target. Then, inflict 1 mortal damage on each other enemy unit within the target’s combat range. If the chanting roll was 7+, inflict D3 mortal damage on the target instead of 1.
- **Ever Turning**（Your Hero Phase / 詠唱/詠誦値 5）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **CYCLE OF CORRUPTION PRIEST** to chant this prayer, then make a chanting roll of D6.
  - 効果: The next **CYCLE** ability in the sequence is ripe for the rest of the battle round instead of the current **CYCLE** ability. If the chanting roll was 12+, you can pick which **CYCLE** ability is ripe.
