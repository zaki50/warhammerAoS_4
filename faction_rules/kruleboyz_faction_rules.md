## バトル特性


### Kruleboyz Battle Traits（出典: Kruleboyz）

Kruleboyz armies can use the following abilities:

- **Lethal Surprise**（Once Per Turn (Army), Any Charge Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick an enemy unit that charged this turn and is in combat with any friendly **KRULEBOYZ** units to be the target, then make a dirty trick roll.
  - 効果: Inflict D3 mortal damage on the target. In addition, for the rest of the turn, the target’s weapons cannot be affected by the **Charge (+1 Damage)** weapon ability.
- **Noisy Racket**（Once Per Turn (Army), Any Combat Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick an enemy unit in combat with any friendly **KRULEBOYZ** units to be the target, then make a dirty trick roll.
  - 効果: The target has **STRIKE-LAST** for the rest of the turn.
- **Venom-encrusted Weapons**（Once Per Turn (Army), Any Hero Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **KRULEBOYZ INFANTRY** unit to be the target, then make a dirty trick roll.
  - 効果: Until the start of your next turn, the target’s attacks score critical hits on unmodified hit rolls of 5+.
- **Dirty Tricks**（Passive）
  - 効果: The effect of a **DIRTY TRICK** ability is only applied if you make a successful dirty trick roll. The success of a dirty trick roll depends on the number of **DIRTY TRICK** abilities your army has already used this battle round.
- **Sneaky Sneakin'**（Once Per Turn (Army), Any Hero Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **KRULEBOYZ INFANTRY** unit that has 10 or fewer models, is not in combat and has not been the target of a **DIRTY TRICK** ability this turn to be the target, then make a dirty trick roll.
  - 効果: Remove the target from the battlefield and set it up again wholly within 3" of a terrain feature and more than 9" from all enemy units.

## バトルフォーメーション


### Badmouthing Baiterz

- **Krule Mockery**（Once Per Turn (Army), Your Shooting Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **KRULEBOYZ** unit to be the **Insult Hurla**, pick a visible enemy unit within 18" of it to be the target, then make a dirty trick roll.
  - 効果: Until the start of your next turn, each time the target uses a **MOVE** ability and does not end that move closer to the **Insult Hurla** than it was at the start of that move, inflict D6 mortal damage on the target after that **MOVE** ability has been resolved.

### Kruleboyz Klaw

- **Swamp Shroud**（Passive）
  - 効果: Friendly **KRULEBOYZ** units that are wholly within 3" of a terrain feature are not visible to enemy units that are more than 12" away from them.

### Light Finga

- **Ded Sneaky**（Passive）
  - 効果: You can use the ‘Sneaky Sneakin'’ **DIRTY TRICK** ability twice per turn instead of only once per turn.

### Middul Finga

- **Far-killa Bolts**（Your Shooting Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick up to 3 friendly **Man‑skewer Boltboyz** or **Beast-skewer** **Killbow** units to be the targets.
  - 効果: For the rest of the turn, add 3" to the Range characteristic of the targets’ ranged weapons.

### Swamphorde Bullies

- **Gangin' Up**（Once Per Turn (Army), Any Combat Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **KRULEBOYZ** unit to be the target. That unit must be in combat with an enemy unit that is in combat with another friendly **KRULEBOYZ** unit. **Kragnos** cannot be picked to be the target.
  - 効果: For the rest of the turn, add 1 to the Attacks characteristic of the target’s melee weapons.

### Trophy Finga

- **Meanest Beasts**（Passive）
  - 効果: **Companion** weapons used by friendly **KRULEBOYZ** units have **Crit (2 Hits)**.

## 英雄特性


### Kunnin' Warlords（出典: Kruleboyz）

**HERO** only

- **Egomaniak**（Passive / 10pt）
  - 効果: While this unit is within the combat range of a friendly non-**HERO KRULEBOYZ INFANTRY** unit: • This unit has **WARD (4+)**. • Each time you make a successful ward roll for this unit, allocate 1 damage point to a friendly non-**HERO KRULEBOYZ INFANTRY** unit within this unit’s combat range after the damage sequence for this unit has been resolved (ward rolls cannot be made for those damage points).
- **Slippery Skumbag**（Any Combat Phase）
  - 効果: If this unit is in combat, roll a dice. On a 3+, it can immediately use the ‘Retreat’ ability as if it were your movement phase without any mortal damage being inflicted on it.
- **Backstabba**（End of Any Turn）
  - 宣言: Pick an enemy **HERO** in combat with both this unit and another friendly **KRULEBOYZ** unit to be the target.
  - 効果: Inflict D3 mortal damage on the target. Add 1 to the mortal damage inflicted for each friendly **KRULEBOYZ** unit in combat with the target in addition to the first two **KRULEBOYZ** units.

## アーティファクト・オブ・パワー


### Krule Artefacts（出典: Kruleboyz）

**HERO** only

- **Swamp Staff**（Passive）
  - 効果: If this unit is not a **WIZARD**, it can use the ‘Unbind’ ability as if it had **WIZARD (1)**. Otherwise, add 1 to this unit’s power level.
- **Eye-biter Ash**（Once Per Battle, Any Combat Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick an enemy unit in combat with this unit to be the target.
  - 効果: Subtract 1 from hit rolls and wound rolls for the target’s attacks for the rest of the turn.
- **Mork's Eye Pebble**（Once Per Battle, Reaction: Opponent declared an ATTACK ability）
  - 効果: For the rest of the turn, friendly **KRULEBOYZ** units have **WARD** **(5+)** while they are wholly within 12" of this unit.

### Krule Artefacts（出典: Scourge of Aqshy: Kruleboyz）

**HERO** only

- **Tattermask**（Passive）
  - 効果: If the unmodified hit roll for an attack that targets this unit is 1-3, that attack fails and the attack sequence ends.
- **Spell in a Rattle**（Once Per Battle, Any Hero Phase）
  - 効果: This unit can use a non‐**SUMMON SPELL** ability from a spell lore you picked during army composition as if it had **WIZARD (1)**. Instead of making a casting roll for that spell, the casting roll is 9 and cannot be modified. That spell cannot be unbound.
- **Woggly Bogstikk**（Your Hero Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a visible enemy unit within 12" of this unit to be the target.
  - 効果: Roll a dice. If the roll exceeds the target’s Control characteristic, inflict an amount of mortal damage on the target equal to the roll.

## その他の強化


### Kruleboyz Monstrous Traits（出典: Scourge of Ghyran: Kruleboyz）

Monstrous Traits are enhancements that can only be given to **MONSTERS**.

- **Tough 'Un**（Passive）
  - 効果: Subtract 1 from the Rend characteristic of weapons used for attacks that target this unit.
- **Sneaky 'Un**（Once Per Battle, Enemy Hero Phase）
  - 効果: This unit can use the ‘Normal Move’ or ‘Retreat’ ability as if it were your movement phase. In addition, no mortal damage is inflicted on this unit by **RETREAT** abilities this phase.
- **Mean 'Un**（Passive）
  - 効果: Add 1 to hit rolls for attacks made with this unit’s **Companion** weapons.

### Kunnin' Creatures（出典: Scourge of Aqshy: Kruleboyz）

**KRULEBOYZ MONSTER** only

- **Spittin' 'Un**（Passive / 20pt）
  - 効果: This unit is armed with the ranged weapon below, where **X** is equal to your **fury level**. Ranged Weapon **Caustic Saliva** Rng: 12" Atk: X Hit: 4+ Wnd: 3+ Rnd: 2 Dmg: D3
- **Sly 'Un**（Once Per Battle, End of Any Turn / 10pt）
  - 効果: Remove this unit from the battlefield and set it up again on the battlefield more than 9" from all enemy units.
- **Quick 'Un**（Passive / 20pt）
  - 効果: Add half your **fury level** (rounding up) to run rolls and charge rolls for this unit.
