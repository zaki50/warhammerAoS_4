## バトル特性


### The Gardeners of Nurgle Battle Traits（出典: Army of Renown: The Gardeners of Nurgle）

- **The Garden Grows**（Once Per Turn (Army), Your Hero Phase）
  - 効果: Set up a **Feculent Gnarlmaw** on the battlefield more than 3" from all enemy units, objectives and other terrain features.
- **Transplanting**（Once Per Turn (Army), Your Movement Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **Feculent Gnarlmaw** to be the target.
  - 効果: Remove the target from the battlefield and set it up again within 3" of a friendly **GARDENERS OF NURGLE HERO** and more than 3" from all enemy units, objectives and other terrain features.
- **The Head Gardener**（Once Per Battle (Army), Your Movement Phase）
  - 効果: The friendly **Horticulous Slimux** can use his ‘Cultivating the Garden of Nurgle’ ability this phase even if there are 1 or more friendly **Feculent Gnarlmaws** on the battlefield.
- **Putrescent Sap**（Once Per Turn (Army), Your Combat Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick each enemy unit within 3" of any friendly **Feculent** **Gnarlmaws** to be the targets.
  - 効果: Roll a D3 for each target. On a 2+, inflict an amount of mortal damage on the target equal to the roll.
- **Foul Fauna**（Passive）
  - 効果: Add 2" to the Move characteristic of friendly **GARDENERS OF NURGLE** **Beasts of Nurgle** and **Plague Drones** units while they are wholly within 6" of any friendly **Feculent** **Gnarlmaws**.
- **Sickly Sores**（Passive）
  - 効果: While a friendly **GARDENERS OF NURGLE INFANTRY** unit is wholly within 12" of a friendly **Feculent Gnarlmaw**, ignore the first damage point that would be allocated to it in each phase.

## 英雄特性


### The Gardeners of Nurgle Heroic Trait（出典: Army of Renown: The Gardeners of Nurgle）

(**HERO** only)

- **Foetid Orchardist**（Passive）
  - 効果: Add 1 to hit rolls for this unit’s combat attacks while it is wholly within 7" of any friendly **Feculent Gnarlmaws**.
