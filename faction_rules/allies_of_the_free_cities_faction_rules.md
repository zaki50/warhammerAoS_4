## バトル特性


### Allies of the Free Cities Battle Traits（出典: Army of Renown: Allies of the Free Cities）

- **Form up, Allies!**（Start of Battle Round）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **ALLIES OF THE FREE CITIES** unit to use this ability, then pick 2 other visible friendly **ALLIES OF THE FREE CITIES** units wholly within 12" of it to be the targets. The unit using this ability and the targets: • cannot be **under orders**. • must each be a different unit type.
  - 効果: For the rest of the battle round, the unit using this ability and the targets are **under orders**. **Designer’s Note:** *‘Unit type’ refers to the name of the warscroll (Core Rules, 4.0), e.g. ‘Freeguild Cavalier-Marshal’ or ‘Allies of the Free Cities Auric Hearthguard’.*
- **Forces of Order**（Passive）
  - 効果: While 3 friendly **ALLIES OF THE FREE CITIES** units are wholly within 12" of each other and 1 of those units has the **AELF** keyword, 1 of those units has the **DUARDIN** keyword and 1 of those units has the **SIGMARITE** keyword: • Those units have **WARD (5+)** against shooting attacks. • Add 3 to those units’ control scores. • Add 1 to wound rolls for those units’ combat attacks.

## 英雄特性


### Allies of the Free Cities Heroic Trait（出典: Army of Renown: Allies of the Free Cities）

(**HERO** only)

- **Respected Commander**（Reaction: You declared the ‘Redeploy’ command for a visible **ALLIES OF THE FREE CITIES** unit wholly within 18" of this unit）
  - 効果: If you roll a 1-3 when determining the distance that unit can move, you can use a value of 4 instead.

## アーティファクト・オブ・パワー


### Allies of the Free Cities Artefact of Power（出典: Army of Renown: Allies of the Free Cities）

(**HERO** only)

- **Signet of the City**（Once Per Battle (Army), Any Hero Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick this unit and each visible friendly **ALLIES OF THE FREE CITIES** unit wholly within 18" of this unit to be the targets.
  - 効果: For the rest of the turn: • The targets are **under orders**. • The targets have **WARD (6+)**. • The targets have a maximum control score of 1.

## ロア（呪文・祈祷・顕現）


### Allies of the Free Cities Prayer Lore

- **Divine Mandate**（Your Hero Phase / 詠唱/詠誦値 5）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **ALLIES OF THE FREE CITIES PRIEST** to chant this prayer, pick a visible friendly **ALLIES OF THE FREE CITIES** unit wholly within 12" of them to be the target, then make a chanting roll of D6.
  - 効果: If the chanting roll was 10+, you can pick another eligible unit to be a second target. Until the start of your next turn, enemy units have a maximum control score of 1 while they are in combat with any of the targets.

### Allies of the Free Cities Spell Lore

- **These Realms are Ours**（Your Hero Phase / 詠唱/詠誦値 7）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **ALLIES OF THE FREE CITIES WIZARD** to cast this spell, pick an objective within 18" of them to be the target, then make a casting roll of 2D6.
  - 効果: For the rest of the turn, melee weapons used by friendly **ALLIES OF THE FREE CITIES** units have **Charge (+1 Damage)** for attacks that target enemy units that are contesting the target objective.
