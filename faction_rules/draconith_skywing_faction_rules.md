## バトル特性


### Draconith Skywing Battle Traits（出典: Army of Renown: Draconith Skywing）

- **Thunderous Roar**（Once Per Turn (Army), Any Combat Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **DRACONITH SKYWING MONSTER** that has not used any **RAMPAGE** abilities this turn to use this ability. Then, pick up to 3 enemy units in combat with it to be the targets.
  - 効果: Roll a dice for each target. On a 3+, subtract 5 from that target’s control score for the rest of the turn.
- **Aetheric Cyclone**（Once Per Turn (Army), Any Combat Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **DRACONITH SKYWING MONSTER** that has not used any **RAMPAGE** abilities this turn to use this ability. Then, pick an enemy unit in combat with it to be the target.
  - 効果: Roll a dice. On a 3+, subtract 1 from wound rolls for the target’s attacks for the rest of the turn.
- **Exemplars of Fury**（Passive）
  - 効果: Add 1 to the Attacks characteristic of **Companion** weapons used by friendly **DRACONITH SKYWING HEROES** while they are within 6" of 2 or more friendly **Stormdrake Guard** units.
- **Shields of the Warden**（Passive）
  - 効果: While any friendly **Stormdrake Guard** units are within a friendly **Ionus Cryptborn**’s combat range: • That friendly **Ionus Cryptborn** has **WARD (4+)**. • Each time you make a successful ward roll for that friendly **Ionus Cryptborn**, allocate 1 damage point to a friendly **Stormdrake Guard** unit within its combat range after the damage sequence for that friendly **Ionus Cryptborn** has been resolved (ward rolls cannot be made for those damage points).
