## バトル特性


### Vanari Paragons Battle Traits（出典: Army of Renown: Vanari Paragons）

- **Rewards of Valour**（Once Per Battle (Army), Deployment Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly non-**UNIQUE VANARI HERO** that does not have an artefact of power to be the target.
  - 効果: Give the target 1 artefact of power from the 'Vanari Paragons' Army of Renown rules. **Designer's Note:** *This artefact of power is given to the* **HERO** *during the deployment phase, not during army composition.*
- **Labour of Might**（Once Per Battle (Army), End of Any Turn）
  - 宣言: You can only use this ability if 3 or more enemy units have been destroyed this battle.
  - 効果: Add 1 to the number of **labours** you have completed. For the rest of the battle, add 1 to wound rolls for combat attacks made by friendly **LUMINETH REALM-LORDS** units.
- **Labour of Control**（Once Per Battle (Army), End of Any Turn）
  - 宣言: You can only use this ability if there is at least 1 different friendly unit within 6" of each battlefield edge.
  - 効果: Add 1 to the number of **labours** you have completed. For the rest of the battle, add 2" to the Move characteristic of friendly **LUMINETH REALM-LORDS** units.
- **Labour of Precision**（Once Per Battle (Army), End of Any Turn）
  - 宣言: You can only use this ability if there are no enemy units with an artefact of power on the battlefield.
  - 効果: Add 1 to the number of **labours** you have completed. For the rest of the battle, add 10 to the control scores of friendly **LUMINETH REALM‑LORDS** units.
- **Blessing of Tyrion**（Once Per Battle (Army), End of Any Turn）
  - 宣言: You can only use this ability if you have completed 3 **labours**.
  - 効果: For the rest of the battle, friendly **LUMINETH REALM-LORDS** units have **WARD (5+)**.

## 英雄特性


### Vanari Paragons Heroic Trait（出典: Army of Renown: Vanari Paragons）

**HERO** only

- **Master Duellist**（Any Combat Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a visible enemy **HERO** in combat with this unit to be the target.
  - 効果: Roll a dice. On a 4+: • If the target has an artefact of power, it no longer has that artefact of power. • The target has **STRIKE‑LAST** for the rest of the turn.

### Vanari Paragons Artefacts of Power（出典: Army of Renown: Vanari Paragons）

**HERO** only

- **Swiftfeather Talisman**（Your Movement Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **LUMINETH REALM-LORDS** unit wholly within 12" of this unit to be the target.
