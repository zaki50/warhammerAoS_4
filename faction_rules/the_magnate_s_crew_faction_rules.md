## バトル特性


### The Magnate's Crew Battle Traits（出典: Army of Renown: The Magnate's Crew）

- **Loyalty to the Magnate**（Passive）
  - 効果: While a friendly **Brokk Grungsson** is wholly within the combat range of another friendly **MAGNATE’S CREW INFANTRY** unit, both those units have **WARD (5+)**.
- **Pay Your Debts**（Once Per Battle (Army), Deployment Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick an enemy unit on the battlefield or in reserve to be **indebted**.
  - 効果: Add 1 to wound rolls for combat attacks made by friendly **MAGNATE’S CREW** units that target the **indebted** unit.
- **Bold Moves**（Once Per Battle (Army), Your Movement Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick any number of friendly **MAGNATE’S CREW INFANTRY** units that are in combat to be the targets. Make an **emergency launch roll** of 3D6 for each target.
  - 効果: Each target can move a distance up to the value of its **emergency launch roll**. It **can** move through the combat ranges of any enemy units but **cannot** end that move in combat with an enemy unit that it was not in combat with at the start of the phase. The target(s) cannot use **CHARGE** abilities for the rest of the turn.
- **Cutting-Edge Endrinpacks**（Once Per Turn (Army), Your Movement Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick up to 2 friendly **MAGNATE’S CREW INFANTRY** units that are wholly within 9" of a friendly **MAGNATE’S CREW SKYVESSEL** to be the targets.
  - 効果: For the rest of the turn, each target can use **CHARGE** abilities even if it used a **RUN** ability in the same turn.
- **Invictunite-lined Hull**（Once Per Battle (Army), Enemy Hero Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **MAGNATE’S CREW SKYVESSEL** to be the target.
  - 効果: Roll a D3. For the rest of the turn, subtract **X** from the Rend characteristic of weapons used for attacks that target that **SKYVESSEL**, where **X** is equal to the roll.
- **Constant Barrage**（Once Per Turn (Army), Your Shooting Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick an enemy unit that had any damage points allocated to it this phase by a friendly **MAGNATE’S CREW** unit’s attacks to be the target.
  - 効果: Until the start of your next turn, each time the target ends a move within 10" of any friendly **MAGNATE’S CREW** units, roll a D3. On a 2+, inflict an amount of mortal damage on the target equal to the roll.

## 英雄特性


### The Magnate's Crew Heroic Traits（出典: Army of Renown: The Magnate's Crew）

**HERO** only

- **Master of Discipline**（Passive）
  - 効果: Each time a friendly **MAGNATE’S CREW INFANTRY** unit wholly within 12" of this unit uses the ‘Redeploy’ command, you can re-roll the dice.
- **Flanking Fire**（Passive）
  - 効果: Add 1 to hit rolls for shooting attacks made by friendly **MAGNATE’S CREW SKYVESSELS** that target an enemy unit in combat with this unit.
