## バトル特性


### Matriarch's Mob Battle Traits（出典: Army of Renown: Matriarch's Mob）

- **I've Heard She Can...**（Once Per Turn (Army), Your Hero Phase）
  - 効果: Pick 1 of the following effects: ***... Shoot Fire From Her Eyes!:*** Inflict D3 mortal damage on each visible enemy unit within 12" of the friendly **Ma Maegran.** ... ***Turn You Into a Human!:*** Pick a visible friendly unit wholly within 12" of the friendly **Ma Maegran.** Until the start of your next turn, enemy units have a maximum control score of 1 while they are in combat with that unit. ***... Make Ya Big and Strong!:*** Pick another visible friendly unit wholly within 12" of the friendly **Ma Maegran.** Add 1 to the Attacks characteristic of that unit's weapons for the rest of the turn. . . . ***Whiz the Biggest Bangers!:*** Add 1 to casting rolls for the friendly **Ma Maegran** until the start of your next turn.
- **Oi! Gerroff!**（Once Per Battle (Army), Any Combat Phase）
  - 宣言: If the friendly **Ma Maegran** is in combat and damaged, pick up to 3 friendly **Mancrusher Gargants** on the battlefield that are visible to her to be the targets.
  - 効果: Each target has **STRIKE-FIRST** for the rest of the turn.
- **Summon the Herd**（Once Per Battle (Army), End of Any Turn）
  - 宣言: If the friendly **Ma Maegran** is on the battlefield, pick each enemy **non-FLY non-MONSTER** unit that is visible to her to be the targets.
  - 効果: Roll a number of dice for each target equal to the current battle round number. For each 4+, allocate **1** mortal damage to the target (ward rolls cannot be made for those damage points).
- **Don't Let Nuffin' Happen To Her!**（Passive）
  - 効果: While the friendly **Ma Maegran** is within the combat range of another friendly **LITTLE** unit: • She has **WARD (5+).** • Each time you make a successful ward roll for her, allocate 1 mortal damage to another friendly **LITTLE** unit within her combat range after the damage sequence for her has been resolved (ward rolls cannot be made for those damage points). • Ignore negative modifiers to hit rolls and wound rolls for that other friendly **LITTLE** unit's combat attacks.

## その他の強化


### Marks of the Chooser's Chosen（出典: Army of Renown: Matriarch's Mob）

Marks of the Chooser's Chosen are unique enhancements that can be given to **Mancrusher Gargants.** A unit can only have 1 Mark of the Chooser's Chosen.

- **Butt of the Lash**（Any Combat Phase）
  - 効果: If this unit is wholly within 12" of a friendly **Ma Maegran,** remove this unit from the battlefield and set it up again within her combat range. This unit can only be set up in combat with enemy units that are in combat with the friendly **Ma Maegran.**
- **Ghyranite Whirlstone**（End of Any Turn）
  - 宣言: Pick a visible friendly **MATRIARCH'S MOB** unit wholly within 12" of this unit to be the target.
  - 効果: **Heal (D3)** the target.

### Matriarch's Mob Spell Lore

- **A Shepherd's Watchful Eye**（Your Hero Phase / 詠唱/詠誦値 6）
  - 宣言: Pick the friendly **Ma Maegran** to cast this spell, then make a casting roll of 2D6.
  - 効果: Until the start of your next turn, friendly **MATRIARCH'S MOB** units have **WARD (6+)** while they are wholly within 12" of and visible to the caster.
- **Mystical Demands**（Your Hero Phase / 詠唱/詠誦値 7）
  - 宣言: Pick the friendly **Ma Maegran** to cast this spell, pick a visible friendly **MATRIARCH'S MOB** unit wholly within 18" of her to be the target, then make a casting roll of 2D6.
  - 効果: If the target is in combat, it can use the 'Retreat' ability as if it were your movement phase. If the target is not in combat, it can use the 'Normal Move' ability as if it were your movement phase. In both cases, the target must end that move no further from the caster than it was at the start of the move.
- **Sic 'Em!**（Your Hero Phase / 詠唱/詠誦値 8）
  - 宣言: Pick the friendly **Ma Maegran** to cast this spell, pick a visible friendly **MATRIARCH'S MOB** unit wholly within 12" of her to be the target, then make a casting roll of 2D6.
  - 効果: Add 1 to the Damage characteristic of the target's melee weapons, including **Companion** weapons, for the rest of the turn.
