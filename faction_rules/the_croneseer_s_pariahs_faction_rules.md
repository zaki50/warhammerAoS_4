## 戦闘特性


### The Croneseer’s Pariahs Battle Traits（出典: Army of Renown: The Croneseer's Pariahs）

- **Guided by Morai-Heg**（Passive）
  - 効果: Add 1 to the Rend characteristic of combat attacks made by friendly non-**HERO INFANTRY** units while they are wholly within 9" of a friendly **Krethusa**.
- **Skilled Skirmishers**（Once Per Turn (Army), Any Charge Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **CRONESEER’S PARIAHS CAVALRY** unit that has not charged this turn and is in combat with an enemy unit that charged this turn to be the target.
  - 効果: Roll a dice. On 3+, the target can immediately use a **RETREAT** ability as if it were your movement phase. In addition, no mortal damage is inflicted on the target by that **RETREAT** ability.
- **Plumes of Auspicious Smoke**（Passive）
  - 効果: Friendly **Slaughter Queen on Cauldron of Blood** or **Hag Queen on Cauldron of Blood unit** units are either **empty** or **full**. They start the battle **empty**. While a friendly **Slaughter Queen on Cauldron of Blood** or **Hag Queen on Cauldron of Blood unit** unit is **full**: • That **Slaughter Queen on Cauldron of Blood** or **Hag Queen on Cauldron of Blood unit** has **WARD (4+)** against damage points inflicted by shooting attacks. • Subtract 1 from hit rolls for shooting attacks that target friendly **CRONESEER’S PARIAHS** units while they are wholly within 9" of that **Slaughter Queen on Cauldron of Blood** or **Hag Queen on Cauldron of Blood unit**.
- **Laden with Prophecy**（End of Any Turn）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **empty Slaughter Queen on Cauldron of Blood** or **Hag Queen on Cauldron of Blood unit**.
  - 効果: If any enemy models were slain this turn by that **Slaughter Queen on Cauldron of Blood** or **Hag Queen on Cauldron of Blood unit'**&#x73; combat attacks, it becomes **full**.
- **The Blood Reveals All**（Once Per Battle (Army), Your Hero Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **CRONESEER’S PARIAHS HERO** within the combat range of a friendly **empty** **Slaughter Queen on Cauldron of Blood** or **Hag Queen on Cauldron of Blood unit**. Then, pick another unit within the combat range of that **HERO** to be the target.
  - 効果: Roll a dice. Allocate a number of damage points to the target equal to the roll (ward rolls cannot be made for those damage points). Then, that **Slaughter Queen on Cauldron of Blood** or **Hag Queen on Cauldron of Blood unit** becomes **full**.
