## バトル特性


### Sons of Behemat Battle Traits（出典: Faction Pack: Sons of Behemat）

Sons of Behemat armies can use the following abilities:

- **Jump Up and Down**（Once Per Turn (Army), Any Combat Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **MEGA‑GARGANT** or **GARGANT** unit that has not used a **RAMPAGE** ability this turn to use this ability, then pick an enemy **INFANTRY** unit in combat with it to be the target.
  - 効果: Roll a dice. On a 3+, roll a dice for each model in the target unit. If the unit using this ability is a **GARGANT**, inflict 1 mortal damage on the target for each 6. If it is a **MEGA-GARGANT,** inflict 1 mortal damage on the target for each 5+.
- **Gargant Charge**（Any Charge Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **MEGA-GARGANT** or **GARGANT** unit that charged this turn to use this ability, then pick an enemy unit that has not been picked as the target of this ability this turn and that is in combat with that unit to be the target. **Designer’s Note**: *This ability can be used by any number of friendly **MEGA-GARGANT** and **GARGANT** units that charged in each turn.*
  - 効果: Inflict D3 mortal damage on the target.
- **Colossal Slam**（Once Per Turn (Army), Any Combat Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **MEGA‐GARGANT** that has not used a **RAMPAGE** ability this turn to use this ability, then pick an enemy **MONSTER** or **WAR MACHINE** that has a unit size of 1 and is within 1" of it to be the target.
  - 効果: Roll a dice. On a 3+, remove the target from the battlefield and set it up again within 1" of the unit using this ability. Then, inflict D3 mortal damage on the target.
- **Earthshaking Roar**（Once Per Turn (Army), Any Combat Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **MEGA‑GARGANT** or **GARGANT** unit that has not used a **RAMPAGE** ability this turn to use this ability, then pick an enemy unit in combat with it to be the target.
  - 効果: Roll a dice. On a 3+, the target cannot use commands for the rest of the turn.
- **Timberrrrr!**（Passive）
  - 効果: Each time a friendly **MEGA‑GARGANT** or **GARGANT** model is slain, before removing it from play, the players must roll off. The winner picks a point on the battlefield within 3" of that model. Inflict D3 mortal damage on each unit (friendly and enemy) within 3" of that point that has a lower Health characteristic than that model.

## バトルフォーメーション


### Big Toes

- **Lofty Louts**（Once Per Battle, Deployment Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **Mancrusher Gargant** unit to be the target.
  - 効果: For the rest of the battle, add 1 to hit rolls for combat attacks made by friendly **GARGANT** units while they are wholly within 12" of the target and not wholly within 12" of any friendly **MEGA-GARGANT** units.

### Boss Tribe

- **Big Mouth**（Once Per Turn (Army), Reaction: You declared a FIGHT ability for a friendly MEGA-GARGANT）
  - 効果: Pick a friendly **GARGANT** unit that has not used a **FIGHT** ability this turn and is wholly within 12" of this unit to be the target. The target can be picked to use a **FIGHT** ability immediately after the **FIGHT** ability used by this unit has been resolved.

### Breaker Tribe

- **Besieging Bulk**（Passive）
  - 効果: Friendly **MEGA-GARGANTS** have **WARD (6+)** while they are within the combat ranges of any other friendly **MEGA-GARGANTS**.

### Manskittle Mob

- **Strike! Hur Hur!**（Once Per Turn (Army), Your Shooting Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick up to 2 friendly **MEGA-GARGANT** units within 3" of any terrain features and pick any friendly **GARGANT** units within 3" of any terrain features to be the targets.
  - 効果: Double the Attacks characteristic of each target’s ranged weapons for the rest of the turn. If the target has no ranged weapons, pick an enemy unit within 12" of the target instead and roll a D3. On a 2+, inflict an amount of mortal damage on that enemy unit equal to the roll.

### Stomper Tribe

- **Revel in War**（Passive）
  - 効果: You can pick 2 friendly **MEGA‑GARGANTS** or **GARGANT** units that have not used a **RAMPAGE** ability this turn to use the ‘Jump Up and Down’ ability instead of 1.

### Taker Tribe

- **More Stuff For Me Collection**（Once Per Battle, Deployment Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly non-**UNIQUE SONS OF BEHEMAT HERO** that does not have an artefact of power.
  - 効果: Give that **HERO** 1 artefact of power from ‘Titanic Trophies’. **Designer’s Note:** *This artefact of power is given to the unit during the deployment phase, not during army composition.*

### Big Personalities（出典: Faction Pack: Sons of Behemat）

**HERO** only

- **Monstrously Tough**（Passive）
  - 効果: This unit has a Health characteristic of 40 instead of 35.
- **Rabble Rouser**（Passive）
  - 効果: Add 1 to charge rolls for friendly **SONS OF BEHEMAT** units while they are wholly within 12" of this unit.
- **Furious Temper**（Once Per Battle, Any Combat Phase）
  - 効果: For the rest of the turn: • Add 2 to the Attacks characteristic of this unit’s melee weapons. • Subtract 1 from save rolls for this unit.

### Big Personalities（出典: Scourge of Ghyran: Sons of Behemat）

**HERO** only

- **Grabby**（Passive）
  - 効果: Add 1 to hit rolls for this unit’s combat attacks that target an enemy unit contesting an objective you do not control.
- **Mega-Grump**（Passive）
  - 効果: This unit has **WARD (5+)** while it has 25 or more damage points.
- **Terrifying Hulk**（Once Per Turn, Reaction: Your opponent declared a CHARGE ability for a visible non-MONSTER enemy unit within 12" of this unit）
  - 効果: If this unit is not in combat, roll a dice. On a 3+, you can change one of the dice in that charge roll to a 1.

## アーティファクト・オブ・パワー


### Titanic Trophies（出典: Faction Pack: Sons of Behemat）

**HERO** only

- **Glowy Lantern**（Passive）
  - 効果: This unit can use **SUMMON** abilities from the manifestation lore you picked during army composition as if it had **WIZARD (1)**.
- **Mantle of Tusk and Horns**（Once Per Battle, Your Combat Phase / 10pt）
  - 効果: For the rest of the turn, add 1 to hit rolls for combat attacks made by friendly **SONS OF BEHEMAT** units while they are wholly within 12" of this unit.
- **Glowy Shield of Protectiness**（Passive）
  - 効果: Each time you make an unmodified save roll of 6 for a combat attack that targets this unit, inflict 1 mortal damage on the attacking unit after the **FIGHT** ability has been resolved.
- **Extra-calloused Feet**（Passive / 10pt）
  - 効果: Add 1 to the Attacks characteristic, Rend characteristic and Damage characteristic of this unit’s **Almighty Stomp** or **Almightier Stomp**.
- **Kingslaughter Cowl**（Passive）
  - 効果: Add 1 to the Attacks characteristic of this unit’s melee weapons while this unit is in combat with an enemy **HERO**.
- **Amberbone Totem**（Once Per Battle, Your Charge Phase）
  - 効果: For the rest of the turn, this unit can use **CHARGE** abilities even if it used a **RUN** ability in the same turn.

## その他の強化


### Cracked Heels（出典: Scourge of Aqshy: Sons of Behemat）

Cracked Heels are unique enhancements that can be given to **Mancrusher Gargants**.  A unit can only have 1 Cracked Heel.

- **That Boy's not Right**（Your Hero Phase / 10pt）
  - 効果: Roll a dice. On a 5+, this unit can use a Prayer ability from the prayer lore you picked during army composition as if it had **PRIEST (1)**. Instead of making a chanting roll for that prayer, the chanting roll is 5 and cannot be modified.
- **Up For It**（Passive / 10pt）
  - 効果: This unit’s melee weapons have **Charge (+1 Damage)**.
- **Always on Guard**（Passive / 10pt）
  - 効果: This unit has **WARD (5+)** while it is wholly within 12" of and visible to a friendly **MEGA-GARGANT**.

## ロア（呪文・祈祷・顕現）


### Brodd's Bellows

- **Rock Bottom**（Your Hero Phase / 詠唱/詠誦値 3）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **SONS OF BEHEMAT PRIEST** to chant this prayer, pick a visible friendly **SONS OF BEHEMAT** unit wholly within 12" of them to be the target, then make a chanting roll of D6.
  - 効果: If the chanting roll was 6+, you can pick another eligible unit to be a second target. Until the start of your next turn: •  Ignore the effects of the ‘Battle Damaged’ ability on each target. •  Each target has **WARD (6+)**.
- **The Terrible Toes**（Your Hero Phase / 詠唱/詠誦値 4）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **SONS OF BEHEMAT PRIEST** to chant this prayer, pick a visible enemy unit within 18" of them to be the target, then make a chanting roll of D6.
  - 効果: Roll a dice for each model in the target unit. If the chanting roll was 8+, add 1 to the roll. For each 6+, inflict 1 mortal damage on the target. Then, if any enemy models are slain by this ability, halve the target’s Move characteristic until the start of your next turn.
- **Vocal Tectonics**（Your Hero Phase / 詠唱/詠誦値 5）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **SONS OF BEHEMAT PRIEST** to chant this prayer, pick a visible enemy unit within 12" of them to be the target, then make a chanting roll of D6.
  - 効果: If the chanting roll was 10+, you can pick another eligible unit to be a second target. Until the start of your next turn, each target has **STRIKE-LAST**.
