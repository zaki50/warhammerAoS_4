# Skaven ファクションルール

## バトル特性


### Skaven Battle Traits（出典: Chaos Battletome: Skaven）

Skaven armies can use the following abilities:

- **Too Quick To Hit-hit**（Passive）
  - 効果: No mortal damage is inflicted on friendly **SKAVEN INFANTRY** and **CAVALRY** units by **RETREAT** abilities.
- **Always Three Clawsteps Ahead**（Once Per Turn (Army), Enemy Hero Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly non-**MONSTER SKAVEN** unit that is not in combat and was not set up this turn to use this ability.
  - 効果: That unit can use the 'Normal Move' ability as if it were your movement phase.
- **Splinters of the Vermindoom**（Once Per Battle Round (Army), Start of Battle Round）
  - 宣言: You can use this ability if there are fewer than 3 friendly **Gnawholes** on the battlefield.
  - 効果: Set up a **Gnawhole** on the battlefield more than 9" from all enemy units, more than 3" from all objectives and more than 1" from all other terrain features.
- **The Lurking Vermintide**（Deployment Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **SKAVEN** unit that has not been deployed.
  - 効果: Set up that unit in reserve **in the tunnels below**. It has now been deployed.
- **Gnawhole Ambush**（Your Movement Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **SKAVEN** unit that is **in the** **tunnels below** to use this ability.
  - 効果: Set up that unit wholly within 6" of a friendly **Gnawhole** and more than 9" from all enemy units.

## バトルフォーメーション


### Claw-horde

- **Claw-picked**（Once Per Turn (Army), Your Combat Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick up to 3 friendly **VERMINUS** units that charged this turn to be the targets.
  - 効果: Add 1 to the Rend characteristic of the targets’ melee weapons for the rest of the turn.

### Envoys of the Deepengnaw

- **Will of the Horned Rat**（Passive）
  - 効果: While a friendly **SKAVEN DAEMON** is within the combat range of a friendly non-**HERO SKAVEN INFANTRY** unit: • That **SKAVEN DAEMON** has **WARD (4+)**. • Each time you make a successful ward roll for that **SKAVEN DAEMON**, allocate 1 mortal damage on a friendly non-**HERO SKAVEN INFANTRY** unit within that unit’s combat range after the damage sequence for that unit has been resolved. (ward rolls cannot be made for these damage points).

### Fleshmeld Menagerie

- **Prized Creations**（Once Per Turn (Army), Your Hero Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick up to 3 friendly non-**HERO MOULDER** units to be the targets.
  - 効果: Roll a dice for each target and apply the corresponding effect: **1-2 - *Self-destructive Fury*** Inflict D3 mortal damage on the target. **3-4 - *Rabid Infusion*** Add 1 to the Attacks characteristic of the target’s melee weapons until the start of your next turn. **5-6 -** ***Blinded by Frenzy*** In addition to the effect of ‘Rabid Infusion’, the target has **WARD (5+)** until the start of your next turn.

### Gathering of the Clans

- **Backstabbing Allies**（Once Per Turn (Army), Reaction: You declared the ‘Redeploy’ command for a friendly SKAVEN unit）
  - 効果: Pick up to 2 other friendly **SKAVEN** units that are not in combat and are wholly within 13" of the unit using the ‘Redeploy’ command to be the targets. Each target must have at least one of the following keywords: **VERMINUS**, **SKRYRE**, **MOULDER**, **PESTILENS**, **ESHIN**. After that ‘Redeploy’ command has been resolved, each of the targets can immediately use the ‘Redeploy’ command in an order of your choice without any command points being spent.

### Kill-Pack

- **Kill-Kill! Scurry-Flee!**（Once Per Turn (Army), Deployment Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick an enemy **HERO** to be the target.
  - 効果: For the rest of the battle, if the target has been destroyed, add 2" to the Move characteristic of friendly **ESHIN** units.

### Virulent Procession

- **Corrupted Earth**（Once Per Turn (Army), End of Any Turn）
  - 宣言: Pick up to 3 friendly **PESTILENS** units that are in combat to be the targets.
  - 効果: For each target: • Make a pile-in move. • Then, pick an enemy unit in combat with the target and roll a D3. On a 2+, inflict an amount of mortal damage on that enemy unit equal to the roll.

### Warpcog Convocation

- **Skryre Prototypes**（Once Per Turn (Army), Your Shooting Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick up to 3 friendly **SKRYRE** units to be the targets.
  - 効果: Roll a dice for each target and apply the corresponding effect: **1 -** ***Kaboom!*** Inflict D3 mortal damage on the target. **2-5 - *More Power!*** Add 1 to wound rolls for the target’s shooting attacks for the rest of the turn. **6 - *More-more Power!*** In addition to the effect of ‘More Power!’, add 1 to the Rend characteristic of the target’s ranged weapons for the rest of the turn.

## 英雄特性


### Devious Machinations（出典: Chaos Battletome: Skaven）

**HERO** only

- **Skilled Manipulator**（Passive）
  - 効果: While this unit is within the combat range of a friendly non-**HERO SKAVEN INFANTRY** unit: • This unit has **WARD (4+)**. • Each time you make a successful ward roll for this unit, allocate 1 damage point to a friendly non-**HERO SKAVEN INFANTRY** unit within this unit’s combat range after the damage sequence for this unit has been resolved (ward rolls cannot be made for those damage points).
- **Scurry Away**（Any Combat Phase）
  - 効果: If this unit is in combat, roll a dice. On a 3+, this unit can immediately use the ‘Retreat’ ability as if it were your movement phase.
- **Short-tempered**（Passive）
  - 効果: Add 1 to run rolls and charge rolls for friendly **SKAVEN** units while they are wholly within 13" of this unit.

### Devious Machinations（出典: Scourge of Aqshy: Skaven）

**HERO** only

- **Masterclan Connections**（Reaction: You declared a **SPELL** or **UNBIND** ability for a visible **SKAVEN** unit wholly within 13" of this unit）
  - 効果: Add D3 to that casting roll or unbinding roll.
- **Master of the Swarm**（Passive / 20pt）
  - 効果: Each time an ability returns 1 or more slain models to a friendly **SKAVEN** unit wholly within 13" of this unit, after that ability has been resolved, you can return 1 additional slain model to it.
- **Essence of the Gnaw**（Any Combat Phase / 20pt）
  - 宣言: Pick another visible friendly **SKAVEN** unit wholly within 13" of this unit and in combat to be the target.
  - 効果: For the rest of the turn, this unit has **STRIKE-FIRST** and the target has **STRIKE-LAST**.

### Ruinous Realm-Gnawers（出典: Scourge of Ghyran: Skaven）

**HERO** only

- **Pit Tinkerer**（Passive）
  - 効果: You can re-roll the random characteristic rolls for the Move characteristic of friendly **SKRYRE WAR MACHINES** while they are wholly within 13" of this unit. In addition, each time a friendly **SKRYRE WAR MACHINE** wholly within 18" of this unit uses the ‘Rolling Doom’ or ‘Whirling Doom’ ability, add 1 to the amount of mortal damage inflicted, if any.
- **Devious Underling**（Deployment Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick another friendly **SKAVEN HERO** on the battlefield with a Health characteristic that equals or exceeds this unit’s Health characteristic to be the target.
  - 効果: For the rest of the battle: • While the target has not been slain, friendly **SKAVEN INFANTRY** units have **WARD (6+)** while they are wholly within 13" of this unit. • Once the target has been slain, add 2 to charge rolls for friendly **SKAVEN INFANTRY** units while they are wholly within 13" of this unit.
- **Fleshmeddler**（Your Hero Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **MOULDER** unit wholly within 13" of this unit to be the target.
  - 効果: Pick 1 of the target’s melee weapons. Add 1 to the Rend characteristic of that weapon for the rest of the turn.

**HERO** only

  - 効果: **Heal (D3)** this unit.
- **Warpstone Charm**（Any Combat Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick an enemy unit in combat with this unit to be the target.
  - 効果: Roll a dice. On a 3+, subtract 1 from save rolls for the target for the rest of the turn.

## その他の強化


### Moulder Mutations（出典: Scourge of Aqshy: Skaven）

Moulder Mutations are unique enhancements that can be given to non-**HERO** non-**WAR MACHINE** **SKAVEN** units. A unit can only have 1 Moulder Mutation.

- **Transplanted Brains**（Any Hero Phase / 10pt）
  - 効果: Pick 1 of the following brains. Its effects apply for the rest of the turn: ***Warpstone Addict’s Brain***: • Add 1 to hit rolls for this unit’s combat attacks. • This unit has a maximum control score of 1. ***Warlock Whelp’s Brain***: Add 10 to this unit’s control score.
- **Anabolic Accelerators**（Your Charge Phase / 10pt）
  - 効果: If this unit was set up this turn, when making charge rolls for this unit, add 1 to the number of dice rolled, to a maximum of 3, then remove 1 dice of your choice and use the remaining dice as the charge roll.
- **Serrated Bone Protrusions**（Passive / 10pt）
  - 効果: For each unmodified hit roll of 1 for a combat attack made by an enemy unit that targets this unit, inflict 1 mortal damage on the attacking unit after the **FIGHT** ability has been resolved.

  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **SKAVEN WIZARD** to cast this spell, pick a visible friendly **SKAVEN HERO** wholly within 13" of them to be the target, then make a casting roll of 2D6.
  - 効果: Remove the target from the battlefield and set it up again on the battlefield more than 9" from all enemy units.
- **Warpgale**（Your Hero Phase / 詠唱/詠誦値 6）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **SKAVEN WIZARD** to cast this spell, pick a visible enemy unit within 18" of them to be the target, then make a casting roll of 2D6.
  - 効果: The target has **STRIKE-LAST** for the rest of the turn.
- **Wither**（Your Hero Phase / 詠唱/詠誦値 6）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **SKAVEN WIZARD** to cast this spell, pick a visible enemy unit within 13" of them to be the target, then make a casting roll of 2D6.
  - 効果: Inflict D3 mortal damage on the target.

- **Summon Bell of Doom**（Your Hero Phase / 詠唱/詠誦値 7）
  - 宣言: If there is not a friendly **Bell of Doom** on the battlefield, pick a friendly **SKAVEN WIZARD** to cast this spell, then make a casting roll of 2D6.
  - 効果: Set up a **Bell of Doom** wholly within 13" of the caster, visible to them and more than 9" from all enemy units.
- **Summon Vermintide**（Your Hero Phase / 詠唱/詠誦値 7）
  - 宣言: If there is not a friendly **Vermintide** on the battlefield, pick a friendly **SKAVEN WIZARD** to cast this spell, then make a casting roll of 2D6.
  - 効果: Set up a **Vermintide** wholly within 13" of the caster, visible to them and more than 9" from all enemy units.
- **Summon Warp Lightning Vortex**（Your Hero Phase / 詠唱/詠誦値 7）
  - 宣言: If there is not a friendly **Warp Lightning Vortex** on the battlefield, pick a friendly **SKAVEN WIZARD** to cast this spell, then make a casting roll of 2D6.

### Noxious Prayers

- **Bile-torrent**（Your Hero Phase / 詠唱/詠誦値 4）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **SKAVEN PRIEST** to chant this prayer, pick a visible enemy unit within 13" of them to be the target, then make a chanting roll of D6.
  - 効果: Roll a number of dice equal to the number of models in the target unit. For each 5+, inflict 1 mortal damage on the target. If the chanting roll was 8+, inflict 1 mortal damage on the target for each 4+ instead.
- **Filth-crust**（Your Hero Phase / 詠唱/詠誦値 4）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **SKAVEN PRIEST** to chant this prayer, pick a visible friendly **SKAVEN INFANTRY** unit wholly within 13" of them to be the target, then make a chanting roll of D6.
  - 効果: Add 1 to wound rolls for the target’s combat attacks until the start of your next turn. In addition, if the chanting roll was 8+, the target’s melee weapons have **Crit (Mortal)** until the start of your next turn.
- **Rabid-tough**（Your Hero Phase / 詠唱/詠誦値 5）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **SKAVEN PRIEST** to chant this prayer, pick a visible friendly **SKAVEN INFANTRY** unit wholly within 13" of them to be the target, then make a chanting roll of D6.
  - 効果: Subtract 1 from wound rolls for attacks that target that unit until the start of your next turn. In addition, if the chanting roll was 8+, add 1 to save rolls for the target until the start of your next turn.
