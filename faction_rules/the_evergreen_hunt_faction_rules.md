## バトル特性


### The Evergreen Hunt Battle Traits（出典: Army of Renown: The Evergreen Hunt）

- **Nature Aetheric**（Once Per Battle (Army), Deployment Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a terrain feature that is within friendly territory to be the target.
  - 効果: For the rest of the battle, friendly units are wholly within the **creeping overgrowth** while they are wholly within 6" of the target.
- **Opening Horn Blast**（Once Per Battle Round (Army), Start of Battle Round）
  - 宣言: You must use this ability at the start of the battle round.
  - 効果: Your chords are reset to 0. Then, you gain 1 **chord**: • If a friendly **Belthanos** is on the battlefield. • For each friendly **EVERGREEN HUNT** unit that is wholly within the same large quarter of the battlefield as the quarry. • For each quarry destroyed so far during the battle.
- **Song of the Hunt**（Passive）
  - 効果: The following cumulative effects apply to friendly **EVERGREEN HUNT** units depending on the number of **chords** you have: **1 chord – *Simple***: Add 1 to run rolls and charge rolls for friendly units wholly within the same quarter of the battlefield as the quarry. **2 chords – *Tuneful***: Add 1 to hit rolls and wound rolls for friendly units’ combat attacks that target an enemy unit wholly within the same quarter of the battlefield as the quarry. **3-5 chords – *Melodic***: Add 1 to the Attacks characteristic of melee weapons used by friendly units while they are in combat with the quarry. **6+ chords – *Mellifluous***: While a friendly unit is in combat with the quarry, the quarry has **STRIKE-LAST**.
- **Rhythm of the Chase**（Once Per Battle Round (Army), Start of Battle Round）
  - 宣言: If there is no **quarry** on the battlefield, pick an enemy unit on the battlefield to be the target.
  - 効果: The target is the **quarry** for the rest of the battle.
- **Questing Tendrils**（Once Per Turn (Army), Start of Any Turn）
  - 宣言: Pick the terrain feature that is closest to the **quarry** to be the target.
  - 効果: For the rest of the turn, friendly units are wholly within the **creeping overgrowth** while they are wholly within 6" of the target.
- **A Prize Quarry is Sighted**（Once Per Battle (Army), Your Hero Phase / CP 1）
  - 宣言: Pick an enemy unit within 9" of a friendly **EVERGREEN HUNT** unit to be the target.
  - 効果: The target becomes the **quarry** for the rest of the battle. If there was already a **quarry** on the battlefield when you declared this ability, that unit is no longer the **quarry**.
- **Merciful Strike**（Once Per Turn (Army), Any Combat Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **EVERGREEN HUNT MONSTER** that has not used a **RAMPAGE** ability this turn to use this ability, then pick an enemy unit in combat with it to be the target.
  - 効果: Roll a dice and add the number of damage points the target has. If the result exceeds the target’s Health characteristic, 1 model in the target unit is slain.
- **Abundant Growth**（Once Per Turn (Army), Your Hero Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick each friendly **EVERGREEN HUNT** unit that is wholly within 3" of a terrain feature to be the targets.
  - 効果: **Heal (1)** each target.

### The Evergreen Hunt Heroic Trait（出典: Army of Renown: The Evergreen Hunt）

**HERO** only

- **Sapwood Leader**（Passive）
  - 効果: If you pick this unit to be a target of the 'Abundant Growth’ ability, **Heal (3)** this unit instead of **Heal (1)**.
