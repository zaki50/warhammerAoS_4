## バトル特性


### Trugg's Troggherd Battle Traits（出典: Army of Renown: Trugg's Troggherd）

- **Broken Slumber**（Your Movement Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **TRUGG’S TROGGHERD** unit that is a **living** **landmark**.
  - 効果: Set up that unit anywhere on the battlefield wholly within 3" of a terrain feature and more than 9" from all enemy units.
- **Living Landmark**（Once Per Battle (Army), Deployment Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **TRUGG’S TROGGHERD** unit that has not been deployed.
  - 効果: Set up that unit in reserve as a **living landmark**. It has now been deployed.
- **Moon-toughened Hide**（Passive）
  - 効果: In battle rounds 2 and 3, subtract 1 from the Rend characteristic of melee weapons used for attacks that target friendly **TRUGG’S TROGGHERD** units.
- **Herd Healing**（Passive）
  - 効果: Each time a friendly **TROGGOTH** unit uses a **FIGHT** ability, after that ability has been resolved, **Heal (D3)** that unit.
- **Aura of Haywire Magic**（Passive）
  - 効果: The effects of a friendly **Trugg**’s ‘Malfunctioning Leystone’ ability affect friendly **TRUGG’S TROGGHERD** units anywhere on the battlefield instead of only those wholly within 12" of that friendly **Trugg**.

## 英雄特性


### Trugg's Troggherd Heroic Trait（出典: Army of Renown: Trugg's Troggherd）

**HERO** only

- **Loonstone Teef**（Once Per Battle, Any Combat Phase）
  - 効果: This unit can use 2 **FIGHT** abilities this phase. After the first is used, however, this unit has **STRIKE‑LAST** for the rest of the turn.
