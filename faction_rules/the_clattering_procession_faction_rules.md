## バトル特性


### The Clattering Procession Battle Traits（出典: Army of Renown: The Clattering Procession）

- **Ethereal**（Passive）
  - 効果: Ignore negative modifiers to save rolls for friendly **NIGHTHAUNT** units.
- **The Speed of Death**（Once Per Turn (Army), End of Your Turn）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **CLATTERING PROCESSION** unit that used a **CHARGE** or **RETREAT** ability this turn, is not in combat and is wholly within 9" of a battlefield edge to use this ability.
  - 効果: Remove that unit from the battlefield and set it up again wholly within 9" of a battlefield edge and more than 3" from all enemy units.
- **Spectral Swiftness**（Once Per Turn (Army), Your Movement Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **CLATTERING PROCESSION** unit that is in combat to use this ability.
  - 効果: That unit can move a distance up to its Move characteristic. It can pass through other models and the combat ranges of enemy units, but it cannot end that move in combat.
- **Pitiless Reapers**（Once Per Turn (Army), Your Movement Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **CLATTERING PROCESSION** unit that passed across any enemy units this phase to use this ability, then pick 1 of those enemy units to be the target.
  - 効果: Roll a dice for each model in the **CLATTERING PROCESSION** unit that passed across the target. Add 1 to the roll if any friendly **CLATTERING PROCESSION Black Coaches** passed across the target this phase. For each 6+, inflict 1 mortal damage on the target.

### The Clattering Procession Heroic Trait（出典: Army of Renown: The Clattering Procession）

**HERO** only

  - 効果: For the rest of the turn, the targets can use **CHARGE** abilities even if they used a **RETREAT** ability in the same turn.
