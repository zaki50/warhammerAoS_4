## バトル特性


### Cities of Sigmar Battle Traits（出典: Order Battletome: Cities of Sigmar）

Cities of Sigmar armies can use the following abilities:

- **Form Up!**（Start of Battle Round）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **CITIES OF SIGMAR** unit to use this ability, then pick 2 other visible friendly **CITIES OF SIGMAR** units wholly within 12" of it to be the targets. The unit using this ability and the targets: • cannot be **under orders**. • must each be a different unit type.
  - 効果: For the rest of the battle round, the unit using this ability and the targets are **under orders**. **Designer’s Note:** *‘Unit type’ refers to the name of the warscroll (Core Rules, 4.0), e.g. ‘Freeguild Cavalier-Marshal’ or ‘Cannonade Cogfort’.*
- **Take Aim!**（Once Per Turn (Army), Your Shooting Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly unit that is **under orders** to use this ability.
  - 効果: Pick 1 of that unit’s ranged weapons. Add 1 to hit rolls for shooting attacks made with that weapon for the rest of the turn.
- **Sound the Attack!**（Once Per Turn (Army), Reaction: You declared a CHARGE ability for a unit that is under orders）
  - 効果: You can change the lowest D6 in the charge roll to match the highest D6.
- **Into Them, You Dogs!**（Once Per Turn (Army), Any Combat Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **HERO** that is **under orders** to use this ability. That unit is the **field officer** for the rest of the turn. Then, pick 2 other visible friendly non‑**HERO** units that are **under orders** and wholly within 12" of the **field officer** to be the targets.
  - 効果: For the rest of the turn, the first time the **field officer** uses a **FIGHT** ability, you can pick a target that has not used a **FIGHT** ability this turn. That target is **inspirited** and the other target is **emboldened**. The **inspirited** target can be picked to use a **FIGHT** ability immediately after the **FIGHT** ability used by the **field officer** has been resolved. Then, if the **emboldened** target has not used a **FIGHT** ability this turn, it can be picked to use a **FIGHT** ability immediately after the **inspirited** target’s **FIGHT** ability has been resolved.
- **Hurl Them Back!**（Once Per Turn (Army), Any Combat Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly unit that is **under orders** and in combat with an enemy unit that charged this turn to use this ability.
  - 効果: For the rest of the turn, add 1 to the Rend characteristic of that unit’s melee weapons.

## バトルフォーメーション


### Collegiate Exemplars

- **Masters of the Arcane**（Passive）
  - 効果: Add 1 to arcane manipulation rolls and banishment rolls for friendly **WIZARDS** that are **under orders**. **Designer's Note:** **WIZARDS** *in this battletome use arcane manipulation rolls for a variety of powerful effects.*

### Fearless Exemplars

- **Front-Line Leader**（Once Per Turn (Army), Your Hero Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **CITIES OF SIGMAR HUMAN INFANTRY HERO** to use this ability. Then, you can pick up to 1 friendly non-**HERO** **HUMAN INFANTRY** unit and up to 1 friendly **HUMAN WAR MACHINE** unit that are wholly within 12" of that **HERO** to be the targets.
  - 効果: While the targets are wholly within 12" of that **HERO**, add 1 to the Rend characteristic of the target **INFANTRY** unit’s melee weapons and the target **WAR MACHINE** unit’s ranged weapons until the start of your next turn.

### Grudgebound War Throng

- **I've Seen Worse and Killed Better**（Once Per Turn (Army), Enemy Combat Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick an enemy unit that charged this turn and is in combat with any friendly **CITIES OF SIGMAR DUARDIN** units that did not charge this turn to be the target.
  - 効果: Roll a dice. On a 4+, other than the **Companion** weapon ability, weapon abilities for the target’s combat attacks have no effect for the rest of the turn.

### Stalwart Guardians

- **To the Last**（Passive）
  - 効果: Each time a friendly unit that is **under orders** uses the ‘Rally’ command, you can make 3 additional rally rolls of D6.

### Swift Reinforcements

- **Dawnbringer Crusade**（Once Per Turn (Army), Your Hero Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly non-**HERO** **CITIES OF SIGMAR INFANTRY** unit wholly within friendly territory to use this ability.
  - 効果: If that unit is in combat, it can move 6". It can move through the combat ranges of enemy units but can only end that move in combat with units it was in combat with at the start of that move. It does not have to end the move in combat. If that unit is not in combat, it can move 6". It cannot move into combat during any part of that move.

### Thrall Warhost

- **Have No Mercy**（Once Per Turn (Army), Start of Battle Round）
  - 宣言: Pick a visible enemy unit to be the target.
  - 効果: Until the start of the next battle round, add 1 to wound rolls for combat attacks made by friendly **CITIES OF SIGMAR AELF** units that target that enemy unit while it is damaged or has had any models slain in the same turn.

### Veteran Cannoneers（40pt）

- **Canny Crews**（Once Per Turn (Army), Your Shooting Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick up to 3 friendly **Ironweld Great Cannon** units to be the targets.
  - 効果: For the rest of the turn, each time the target uses a **SHOOT** ability, you can re-roll 1 hit roll, or 1 wound roll, or 1 random characteristic roll for the Damage characteristic.

### Zealous Hordes

- **Throngs of the Faithful**（Once Per Turn (Army), Reaction: Your opponent declared a FIGHT ability）
  - 効果: For the rest of the turn, add 1 to wound rolls for combat attacks made by friendly **SIGMARITE** units that target the enemy unit using that **FIGHT** ability.

## 英雄特性


### Castelite Veterans（出典: Scourge of Ghyran: Cities of Sigmar）

**HERO** only

- **Cosmopolitan Leader**（Your Hero Phase / 20pt）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **CITIES OF SIGMAR INFANTRY** or **CAVALRY** unit wholly within 12" of this unit to be the target. If this unit is **HUMAN**, pick up to 3 eligible units to be the targets instead.
  - 効果: Roll a dice. On a 3+, until the start of your next turn: • If the target is **HUMAN**, add 1 to wound rolls for the target’s combat attacks. • If the target is **DUARDIN**, add 1 to charge rolls for the target. • If the target is **AELF**, add 3 to the target’s control score.
- **Draw Steel!**（Any Combat Phase）
  - 宣言: If this unit charged this turn, pick a friendly **CITIES OF SIGMAR HUMAN** unit that charged this turn and is wholly within 12" of this unit to be the target.
  - 効果: Add 1 to the Attacks characteristic of the target’s melee weapons for the rest of the turn.
- **Astute Tactician**（Your Hero Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **CITIES OF SIGMAR** unit wholly within 12" of this unit to be the target.
  - 効果: For the rest of the turn, add 1 to charge rolls for the target and add 2 to run rolls for the target.

### Sentinels of Order（出典: Order Battletome: Cities of Sigmar）

(**HERO** only excluding **Cannonade Cogfort** and **Conqueror Cogfort**)

- **Stentorian General**（Passive）
  - 効果: Subtract 1 from ward rolls for enemy units while they are in combat with any visible friendly units that are wholly within 12" of this unit and **under orders**.
- **Grizzled Drillmaster**（Passive）
  - 効果: Other visible friendly units have **WARD (6+)** while they are wholly within 12" of this unit and **under orders**.
- **Beloved Leader**（Passive）
  - 効果: Ignore negative modifiers to hit rolls and wound rolls applied by enemy abilities for attacks made by visible friendly units that are wholly within 12" of this unit and **under orders**.

### Sentinels of Order（出典: Scourge of Aqshy: Cities of Sigmar）

**HERO** only, excluding **Cannonade Cogfort** and **Conqueror Cogfort**

- **Pious Veteran**（Passive）
  - 効果: Each time you pick targets while declaring a **PRAYER** ability used by a friendly **CITIES OF SIGMAR PRIEST**, if this unit is eligible to be picked, you can pick it to be an additional target.
- **Bristling Attitude**（Reaction: Opponent declared a **CHARGE** ability for a visible unit within 12" of this unit）
  - 効果: Spend any number of **rage dice**. If your opponent’s **fury level** is lower than yours, they must increase their **fury level** by 1, to a maximum of 7. Then, roll **X** dice, where **X** is the number of **rage dice** spent. For each 3+, subtract 1 from the charge roll.
- **Cosmopolitan Leader**（Your Hero Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick up to 3 visible friendly non‐**WAR MACHINE SIGMARITE** units wholly within 12" of this unit to be the targets.
  - 効果: Spend 1 **rage dice** for each target. If your opponent’s **fury level** is lower than yours, they must increase their **fury level** by 1, to a maximum of 7. Then, add 1 to wound rolls for each target’s combat attacks until the start of your next turn.

## アーティファクト・オブ・パワー


### Treasures of the Cities（出典: Order Battletome: Cities of Sigmar）

(**Hero** only, excluding **Cannonade Cogfort** and **Conqueror Cogfort**)

- **The Last Blade of Embergard**（Deployment Phase / 10pt）
  - 効果: This unit can use this ability while it is in reserve. Pick 1 of this unit’s non-**Companion** melee weapons. Add 2 to that weapon’s Attacks characteristic and Rend characteristic for the rest of the battle.
- **Bones of Saint Ignifus**（Reaction: You declared a **SPELL** or **PRAYER** ability for a visible unit that is wholly within 12" of this unit and under orders）
  - 効果: Add 1 to the casting roll or chanting roll for that ability.
- **The Sphere Celestial**（Your Hero Phase）
  - 宣言: If this unit is not in combat, pick a point on the battlefield within 1" of this unit and more than 3" from all other units (friendly and enemy) and terrain features to be the target point. Then, pick another visible friendly **CITIES OF SIGMAR INFANTRY** or **CAVALRY HERO** that is wholly within 18" of this unit and not in combat to be the target unit.
  - 効果: Remove this unit from the battlefield and set it up again within 1" of the target unit and not in combat. Then, remove the target unit from the battlefield and set it up again within 1" of the target point and not in combat. This unit and the target unit can use **MOVE** abilities in the following movement phase (this is an exception to Core Rules, 24.0).

## その他の強化


### Decorations for Valour（出典: Scourge of Aqshy: Cities of Sigmar）

Decorations for Valour are unique enhancements that can be given to **CITIES OF SIGMAR** units that have the **CHAMPION** keyword.&#x20; A unit can only have 1 Decoration for Valour.

- **Twin-Tailed Comet of Honour**（Passive / 10pt）
  - 効果: While this unit has any champions, add 5 to this unit’s control score.
- **The Living City Cross**（Passive / 10pt）
  - 効果: While this unit has any champions, subtract 1 from hit rolls for shooting attacks that target this unit.
- **Aqshian Order of Merit**（Passive / 10pt）
  - 効果: While this unit has any champions, if an enemy unit in combat with this unit uses the ‘Eruption of Fury’ ability, when resolving attacks as part of that ability, on an unmodified hit roll of 6, do not inflict an additional D3 mortal damage on each unit in combat with that unit.

### Ironweld Innovations（出典: Order Battletome: Cities of Sigmar）

An Ironweld Innovation is a unique enhancement that can be given to **Cannonade Cogforts** and **Conqueror Cogforts**. A unit can only have 1 Ironweld Innovation.

- **Prototype Arco-Combustor Uninhibitor**（Once Per Battle, Your Movement Phase）
  - 効果: For the rest of the turn: • Add 3" to this unit’s Move characteristic. • This unit can use **SHOOT** abilities even if it used a **RETREAT** ability in the same turn. •No mortal damage is inflicted on this unit by **RETREAT** abilities.
- **Grapple-Net Launcher**（Once Per Battle, Enemy Combat Phase）
  - 宣言: If this unit did not charge this turn, pick an enemy unit in combat with this unit and that charged this turn to be the target.
  - 効果: Subtract 1 from the Attacks characteristic of the target’s melee weapons for the rest of the turn.
- **Emergency Bellows**（End of Any Turn）
  - 効果: Remove 1 of this unit’s **heat tokens**.

## ロア（呪文・祈祷・顕現）


### Scriptures of Sigmar

- **Benediction of the High Heavens**（Your Hero Phase / 詠唱/詠誦値 3）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **CITIES OF SIGMAR PRIEST** to chant this prayer, pick another visible friendly **CITIES OF SIGMAR** unit wholly within 12" of them to be the target, then make a chanting roll of D6.
  - 効果: **Heal (D3)** the target and the chanter. If the chanting roll was 7+, **Heal (D3)** each visible friendly **CITIES OF SIGMAR** unit wholly within 12" of the chanter instead.
- **Holy Aegis**（Your Hero Phase / 詠唱/詠誦値 3）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **CITIES OF SIGMAR PRIEST** to chant this prayer, pick a visible friendly **CITIES OF SIGMAR** unit wholly within 12" of them to be the target, then make a chanting roll of D6.
  - 効果: If the chanting roll was 7+, you can pick another eligible unit to be a second target. Until the start of your next turn, ignore the first damage point that would be allocated to each target in each phase.
- **Reckoning of Sinners**（Your Hero Phase / 詠唱/詠誦値 5）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **CITIES OF SIGMAR PRIEST** to chant this prayer, pick a visible friendly **CITIES OF SIGMAR** unit wholly within 12" of them to be the target, then make a chanting roll of D6.
  - 効果: If the chanting roll was 10+, you can pick another eligible unit to be a second target. Until the start of your next turn, enemy units have a maximum control score of 1 while they are in combat with any of the targets.

- **High Star's Light**（Your Hero Phase / 詠唱/詠誦値 6）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **CITIES OF SIGMAR WIZARD** to cast this spell, pick a visible friendly **CITIES OF SIGMAR** unit wholly within 12" of them to be the target, then make a casting roll of 2D6.
  - 効果: Add 1 to the Attacks characteristic of the target’s melee weapons for the rest of the turn.
- **Speed of the Twin-Tailed Comet**（Your Hero Phase / 詠唱/詠誦値 6）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **CITIES OF SIGMAR WIZARD** to cast this spell, then make a casting roll of 2D6.
  - 効果: For the rest of the turn, add 3" to the Move characteristic of friendly non‑**WAR MACHINE CITIES OF SIGMAR** units while they are wholly within 12" of the caster and visible to them.
- **Curse of Scorched Metal**（Your Hero Phase / 詠唱/詠誦値 7）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **CITIES OF SIGMAR WIZARD** to cast this spell, pick a visible enemy unit within 18" of them to be the target, then make a casting roll of 2D6.
  - 効果: Until the start of your next turn, the first time the target uses an **ATTACK** ability, for each unmodified hit roll of 1 for an attack made by the target as part of that **ATTACK** ability, inflict 1 mortal damage on the target after the **ATTACK** ability has been resolved.
