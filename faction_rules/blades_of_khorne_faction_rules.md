## バトル特性


### Blades of Khorne Battle Traits（出典: Chaos Battletome: Blades of Khorne）

Blades of Khorne armies can use the following abilities:

- **Blood-drenched**（Once Per Turn, Reaction: You declared the ‘Skulls for the Skull Throne’ ability）
  - 効果: After that ability has been resolved, if you unlocked a **BLOOD TITHE** ability this turn, pick a friendly **BLADES OF KHORNE** unit to be the target. The following effects apply to the target for the rest of the battle: ***Wild-eyed Brutality:*** Ignore positive modifiers to save rolls for the target. ***Blood Haze:*** If the target is **BLOODBOUND**, its **Blood-hungry** weapons have **Crit (Mortal)**. ***Weapons of Murder:***  If the target is a non-**MONSTER DAEMON** unit, add 1 to the Rend characteristic of its **Blood-hungry** weapons, including **Blood-hungry Companion** weapons. ***Bloodlords Supreme:*** If the target is a **MONSTER**, add 1 to the Damage characteristic of its **Blood-hungry** weapons.
- **Blood for the Blood God**（Passive）
  - 効果: Earn 1 blood tithe point each time a unit (friendly or enemy) is destroyed. Blood tithe points are spent to unlock **BLOOD TITHE** abilities. You can only use **BLOOD TITHE** abilities that are unlocked. Once a **BLOOD TITHE** ability is unlocked, you do not need to spend further blood tithe points to use it.
- **Skulls for the Skull Throne**（Once Per Turn (Army), Start of Any Turn）
  - 効果: Pick a **BLOOD TITHE** ability that you have not unlocked and spend the number of blood tithe points required to unlock that ability (shown in the top-right corner of the ability). That **BLOOD TITHE** ability is now unlocked for the rest of the battle.
- **Murderlust**（Once Per Turn (Army), Reaction: You declared a CHARGE ability）
  - 効果: Roll a dice. You can replace one of the dice in the charge roll with that value. If you do so, inflict D3 mortal damage on the unit using that **CHARGE** ability after that ability is resolved.
- **Divine Scorn**（Once per Turn, Start of Any Turn）
  - 宣言: Pick up to 3 enemy **WIZARDS** or **PRIESTS** within 6" of any friendly **BLADES OF KHORNE** units to be the target(s).
  - 効果: Roll a dice for each target. On a 3+, subtract 1 from the target's power level, to a minimum of 0, until the start of the next turn.
- **Punish the Pacifist**（Once Per Turn (Army), Your Combat Phase）
  - 宣言: Each unit (friendly and enemy) and enemy **MANIFESTATION** that is not in combat is a target.
  - 効果: Roll a dice. On a 1-2, inflict 1 mortal damage on each target. On a 3+, inflict 1 mortal damage on each enemy target.
- **Murder the Mystic**（Once Per Turn (Army), Your Hero Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick up to 4 enemy **MANIFESTATIONS** to be the target(s).
  - 効果: Make a **banishment roll** of 2D6 for each target. If the banishment roll equals or exceeds the banishment value listed on that **MANIFESTATION**'s warscroll, inflict D3 mortal damage on each enemy unit within 3" of it and then it is **banished** and removed from play.
- **Glorious Combat or Naught**（Passive）
  - 効果: Subtract 1 from hit rolls for shooting attacks that target friendly **BLADES OF KHORNE** units.
- **Revel in Battle**（Passive）
  - 効果: Other than the **Companion** weapon ability, weapon abilities for combat attacks made by enemy units that charged this turn and that target a friendly **BLADES OF KHORNE** unit have no effect.
- **Slaughter Triumphant**（Passive）
  - 効果: Add 1 to the Attacks characteristic of weapons used by friendly **BLADES OF KHORNE** units that charged in the same turn.
- **Cleave Wide the Grin of Khorne**（Passive）
  - 効果: Add 1 to hit rolls for combat attacks made by friendly **BLADES OF KHORNE** units.

## バトルフォーメーション


### Bloodbound Warhorde

- **Tireless Conquerors**（Passive）
  - 効果: Add 1 to hit rolls for combat attacks made by friendly **BLOODBOUND** units that target an enemy unit that is contesting an objective you do not control.

### Brass Stampede

- **Drawn to Carnage**（Passive）
  - 効果: Add **X** to charge rolls for friendly **BLADES OF KHORNE** units, where **X** is the number of friendly **BLADES OF KHORNE CAVALRY** or **MONSTER** units that have already charged this phase and that are wholly within 12" of the unit being rolled for.

### Khornate Legion

- **Butchers of Nations**（Once Per Turn (Army), Reaction: You declared a FIGHT ability for a BLADES OF KHORNE DAEMON unit）
  - 効果: Pick a friendly **BLOODBOUND** unit that has not used a **FIGHT** ability this turn and is wholly within 12" of the unit using this ability to be the target. The target can be picked to use a **FIGHT** ability immediately after the **FIGHT** ability used by this unit has been resolved.

### Murderhost

- **Eager Killers**（End of Your Turn）
  - 効果: Each friendly **BLADES OF KHORNE DAEMON** unit that has used a **FIGHT** ability this turn and is not in combat can move D6". It cannot end that move in combat.

### The Goretide

- **Tide of Savagery**（Once Per Turn (Army), Your Movement Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **BLOODBOUND HERO** and up to 2 other friendly **BLOODBOUND** units that are wholly within 12" of that **HERO** to be the targets.
  - 効果: Add 2" to the targets’ Move characteristic for the rest of the turn.

### Tournament of Skulls

- **Bloody Competitors**（Once Per Turn (Army), Your Hero Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly non-**HERO BLOODBOUND** unit and a friendly non-**HERO BLADES OF KHORNE DAEMON** unit to be the targets.
  - 効果: Until the start of your next turn, while the targets are wholly within 12" of each other, add 1 to wound rolls for the targets’ combat attacks.

## 英雄特性


### Slaughterers of Thousands（出典: Chaos Battletome: Blades of Khorne）

**HERO** only.

- **Magical Scorn**（Passive）
  - 効果: Subtract 1 from casting rolls for **WIZARDS** while they are within 12" of this unit. Subtract 1 from chanting rolls for enemy **PRIESTS** while they are within 12" of this unit.
- **Frenzied Taskmaster**（Your Movement Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **BLADES OF KHORNE INFANTRY** or **CAVALRY** unit within this unit's combat range that is not in combat to be the target.
  - 効果: The target can move D6" but cannot end that move in combat. If the target ends that move within this unit's combat range, inflict an amount of mortal damage on the target equal to the roll.
- **Skull Collector**（End of Any Turn）
  - 効果: If any damage points were allocated to an enemy **HERO** by this unit's combat attacks this turn and that enemy **HERO** has been destroyed, add 1 to the Attacks characteristic of this unit's melee weapons for the rest of the battle. This unit can be affected by this ability multiple times and the effects are cumulative.

## アーティファクト・オブ・パワー


### Murderous Artefacts（出典: Chaos Battletome: Blades of Khorne）

**HERO** only

- **Collar of Contempt**（Passive）
  - 効果: This unit can use **UNBIND** abilities as if it had **WIZARD (1).** Each time this unit successfully unbinds a spell, inflict D3 mortal damage on the caster.
- **Ar'gath, The King of Blades**（Passive）
  - 効果: Ward rolls cannot be made for enemy **HEROES** while they are in combat with this unit.
- **Butcher's Blade**（End of Your Turn）
  - 宣言: Pick each enemy unit that had any damage points allocated to it this turn by this unit's combat attacks to be the targets.
  - 効果: Roll a D3 for each target. On a 2+, inflict an amount of mortal damage on the target equal to the roll.

## その他の強化


### Brazen Mutations（出典: Scourge of Aqshy: Blades of Khorne）

Brazen Mutations are unique enhancements that can be given to non-**HERO BLADES OF KHORNE INFANTRY** or **CAVALRY** units. A unit can only have 1 Brazen Mutation.

- **Brass Flesh**（Passive / 20pt）
  - 効果: Unmodified save rolls of 5+ for this unit cannot fail.
- **Blade-Limbs**（Passive / 20pt）
  - 効果: Each time this unit is picked to use the ‘Eruption of Fury’ ability, for each unmodified hit roll of 6 for the attacks made as part of that ability, inflict an additional 3 mortal damage on each enemy unit in combat with this unit instead of D3.
- **Scorpion Tails**（Any Combat Phase / 10pt）
  - 宣言: Pick an enemy unit in combat with this unit to be the target.
  - 効果: • Inflict D3 mortal damage on the target. • If the target unit’s starting size was 1, subtract 1 from wound rolls for its combat attacks for the rest of the turn.

## ロア（呪文・祈祷・顕現）


### Blood Blesssings of Khorne

- **Blood Boil**（Your Hero Phase / 詠唱/詠誦値 4）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **BLADES OF KHORNE PRIEST** to chant this prayer, pick a visible enemy unit within 18" of them to be the target, then make a chanting roll of D6.
  - 効果: Roll a number of dice equal to the number of models in the target unit. For each 5+, inflict 1 mortal damage on the target. In addition, if the chanting roll was 8+, subtract 1 from wound rolls for the target's attacks until the start of your next turn.
- **Final Act of Violence**（Your Hero Phase / 詠唱/詠誦値 4）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **BLADES OF KHORNE PRIEST** to chant this prayer, pick a visible friendly **BLADES OF KHORNE** unit wholly within 12" of them to be the target, then make a chanting roll of D6.
  - 効果: Until the start of your next turn, each time a model in the target unit is slain by a combat attack and that model was in combat with the attacking unit, roll a dice. If the chanting roll was 8+, roll 2 dice instead. For each 5+, inflict 1 mortal damage on the attacking unit after the **FIGHT** ability has been resolved.
- **Uncontrollable Rage**（Your Hero Phase / 詠唱/詠誦値 4）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **BLADES OF KHORNE PRIEST** to chant this prayer, pick a visible friendly unit wholly within 12" of them to be the target, then make a chanting roll of D6.
  - 効果: Until the start of your next turn, the effects of the 'Blood-drenched' ability (see 'Blades of Khorne Battle Traits') apply to the target. In addition, if the chanting roll was 8+, ignore the effect of'Wild-eyed Brutality' on the target.

### Gifts of the Blood God

- **Gift of Apoplexy**（Your Hero Phase / 詠唱/詠誦値 3）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **BLADES OF KHORNE PRIEST** to chant this prayer, pick a visible enemy unit within 18" of them to be the target, then make a chanting roll of D6.
  - 効果: Until the start of your next turn, each time the target uses an ability that is not a **MOVE, CHARGE** or **FIGHT** ability, after that ability is resolved, inflict D3 mortal damage on the target. If the chanting roll was 8+, inflict 3 mortal damage instead of D3.
- **Goreflesh**（Your Hero Phase / 詠唱/詠誦値 3）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **BLADES OF KHORNE PRIEST** to chant this prayer, pick a visible friendly **BLADES OF KHORNE** unit wholly within 12" of them to be the target, then make a chanting roll of D6.
  - 効果: Until the start of your next turn, ignore the first damage point allocated to the target in each phase. If the chanting roll was 8+, you can pick another eligible unit to be a second target.
- **Runes of Loathing**（Your Hero Phase / 詠唱/詠誦値 3）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **BLADES OF KHORNE PRIEST** to chant this prayer, pick a friendly **BLADES OF KHORNE** unit wholly within 12" of them to be the target, then make a chanting roll of D6.
  - 効果: Until the start of your next turn, the target has **WARD (3+)** against damage inflicted by **SPELL** abilities, **PRAYER** abilities and abilities used by **MANIFESTATIONS** (including **FIGHT** abilities). If the chanting roll was 8+, you can pick another eligible unit to be a second target.

### Judgements of Khorne

- **Summon Bleeding Icon**（Your Hero Phase / 詠唱/詠誦値 4）
  - 宣言: If there is not a friendly **Bleeding Icon** on the battlefield, pick a friendly **BLADES OF KHORNE PRIEST** to chant this prayer, then make a chanting roll of D6.
  - 効果: Set up a **Bleeding Icon** wholly within 18" of the chanter and visible to them.
- **Summon Hexgorger Skulls**（Your Hero Phase / 詠唱/詠誦値 4）
  - 宣言: If there is not a friendly **Hexgorger Skulls** invocation on the battlefield, pick a friendly **BLADES OF KHORNE PRIEST** to chant this prayer, then make a chanting roll of D6.
  - 効果: Set up a **Hexgorger Skulls** invocation wholly within 12" of the chanter, visible to them and more than 9" from all enemy units. A **Hexgorger Skulls** invocation has 2 parts that must be set up within 8" of each other.
- **Summon Wrath-axe**（Your Hero Phase / 詠唱/詠誦値 4）
  - 宣言: If there is not a friendly **Wrath-axe** on the battlefield, pick a friendly **BLADES OF KHORNE PRIEST** to chant this prayer, then make a chanting roll of D6.
  - 効果: Set up a **Wrath-axe** wholly within 12" of the chanter, visible to them and more than 9" from all enemy units.

### Proclamations of Murder

- **Gift of Apoplexy**（Your Hero Phase / 詠唱/詠誦値 3）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **BLADES OF KHORNE PRIEST** to chant this prayer, pick a visible enemy unit within 18" of them to be the target, then make a chanting roll of D6.
  - 効果: Until the start of your next turn, each time the target uses an ability that is not a **MOVE** or **FIGHT** ability, after that ability has been resolved, inflict D3 mortal damage o the target. If the chanting roll was 8+, inflict 3 mortal damage instead of D3.
- **Stoke The Flames**（Your Hero Phase / 詠唱/詠誦値 3）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **BLADES OF KHORNE PRIEST** to chant this prayer, pick a visible friendly **BLADES OF KHORNE** unit wholly within 12" of them to be the target, then make a chanting roll of D6.
  - 効果: Until the start of your next turn: • When resolving the damage sequence for enemy units in combat with the target, your opponent cannot spend **rage dice** as part of the ‘Fight Through the Pain’ ability. • If the target is destroyed, you gain 1 **rage dice**. If the chanting roll was 6+, you gain 2 **rage dice** instead.
- **Fetters of Blood**（Your Hero Phase / 詠唱/詠誦値 4）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **BLADES OF KHORNE PRIEST** to chant this prayer, pick a visible enemy unit within 18" of them to be the target, then make a chanting roll of D6.
  - 効果: If the chanting roll was 8+, you can pick another eligible unit to be a second target. Until the start of your next turn: • The targets cannot use **RETREAT** or **RUN** abilities. • The targets cannot be removed from the battlefield by an ability that would allow them to be set up elsewhere on the battlefield or in reserve.
