## バトル特性


### The Equinox Feast Battle Traits（出典: Army of Renown: The Equinox Feast）

- **The Equinox Parade**（Passive）
  - 効果: In the first battle round: •  Add 1 to run rolls for friendly **EQUINOX FEAST** units for each other friendly **EQUINOX FEAST** unit that has used a **RUN** ability this phase. •  Add D6" to the distance friendly **EQUINOX FEAST** units can move when they use a **RETREAT** ability.
- **A Display of Falconry**（Once Per Turn (Army), Any Charge Phase）
  - 宣言: You can only use this ability in the second battle round. Pick each enemy unit that used a **CHARGE** ability this phase to be the targets.
  - 効果: Roll a D3. On a 2+, inflict an amount of mortal damage on the target equal to the roll.
- **The Ritual Dance**（Passive）
  - 効果: In the third battle round, while a friendly **EQUINOX FEAST** unit is in combat with an enemy unit that contains any champions, musicians or standard bearers, it can move 6" instead of 3" when it makes a pile-in move.
- **Eat Your Fill!**（Passive）
  - 効果: In the fourth battle round, each time a friendly **EQUINOX FEAST** unit uses a **FIGHT** ability, after that ability has been resolved: • If that unit is damaged, **Heal (D3)** that unit. •  If that unit is not damaged, return up to 3 slain models to it.
- **Make Merry 'Til the Morn**（Passive）
  - 効果: In the fifth battle round, friendly **EQUINOX FEAST** units have **WARD (4+)** while they are within 3" of 2 or more other visible friendly **EQUINOX FEAST** units.
