## 戦闘特性（Bloodwind Legion Battle Traits）

- **Dreaded Arrival**（Your Movement Phase）
  - 効果: Set up this unit wholly within your territory, within 1" of a battlefield edge and more than 6" from all enemy units.
- **Eye of the Gods**（Once Per Turn, End of Any Turn）
  - 宣言: Pick one of the following friendly units to use this ability: • A unit that is **contesting an objective not controlled by your opponent** and is not in combat. • A unit that **destroyed an enemy unit** this turn.
  - 効果: Roll once on the **Eye of the Gods table** for that unit. That unit gains the **Eye of the Gods** passive ability that corresponds to the roll (the unit keeps all Eye of the Gods abilities gained in previous turns). If the same unit gains the same ability more than once in the battle, the effects are **not** cumulative.

## 連隊アビリティ（Bloodwind Legion Regiment Abilities）

Pick 1 of the following regiment abilities.

- **Fierce Conquerors**（Passive）
  - 効果: Add 3 to the control scores of friendly **Chaos Warriors** units.
- **The Dread Banner**（Once Per Battle, Start of the First Battle Round）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **Chaos Warriors** unit.
  - 効果: You can immediately roll on the Eye of the Gods table for that unit.

## 強化（Bloodwind Legion Enhancements）

Give your general 1 of the following enhancements.

- **Mark of Nurgle**（Passive）
  - 効果: Subtract 1 from wound rolls for combat attacks that target your general.
- **Mark of Tzeentch**（Once Per Battle, Your Movement Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly unit on the battlefield. You cannot pick your general.
  - 効果: Remove that unit from the battlefield and set it up again wholly within 6" of your general and more than 6" from all enemy units. It cannot use **Move** abilities for the rest of the phase.
- **Mark of Slaanesh**（Passive）
  - 効果: Your general has **STRIKE-FIRST**.
- **Mark of Khorne**（Passive）
  - 効果: Add 1 to the Rend characteristic of your general’s melee weapons if they charged in the same turn.
