## レジメントアビリティ（Tyrant's Bellow Regiment Abilities）

Pick 1 of the following regiment abilities.

- **Bred for Toughness**（Passive）
  - 効果: Add 1 to the Health characteristic of your **Ironblaster** and your **Mournfang Pack** unit.
- **Pulverising Girth**（Once Per Phase, Any Charge Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick any number of friendly units that charged this phase.
  - 効果: For each of those units, pick an enemy unit in combat with it and roll a dice. On a 4+, inflict 1 mortal damage on that enemy unit.

## 強化（Tyrant's Bellow Enhancements）

Give your general 1 of the following enhancements.

- **Booming Roar**（Any Combat Phase）
  - 効果: Roll a dice. On a 4+, subtract 1 from hit rolls for attacks made by enemy units this phase while they are within 9" of your general.
- **Longstrider**（Passive）
  - 効果: Your general has a Move characteristic of 8" instead of 6".
- **Blubbergrub**（Once Per Battle, Any Movement Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick either your **Ironblaster** or your **Mournfang Pack** unit if it is within your general’s combat range.
  - 効果: **Heal (D6)** that unit.
- **Flask of Stonehorn Blood**（Once Per Battle, Reaction: Opponent declared an ATTACK ability and targeted your general）
  - 効果: Your general has **WARD (3+)** this phase.
