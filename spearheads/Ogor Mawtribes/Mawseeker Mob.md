## バトル特性（Mawseeker Mob）

- **Frenzied Hunters**（Once Per Battle, Start of Battle Round）
  - 効果: Set up this unit within 1" of a battlefield edge and more than 6" from all enemy units.
- **Wot? No Meat!?**（Passive）
  - 効果: Your general’s Butcher’s Cauldron starts the battle **empty**.
- **Chuck 'Em in the Pot**（End of Any Turn）
  - 宣言: You must use this ability if an enemy unit was destroyed this turn.
  - 効果: Your general’s Butcher’s Cauldron becomes **full**.

## レジメントアビリティ（Mawseeker Mob Regiment Abilities）

Pick 1 of the following regiment abilities.

- **Dish Testers**（Any Combat Phase）
  - 宣言: If your general’s Butcher’s Cauldron is **full**, pick a visible friendly unit wholly within 12" of them to be the target.
  - 効果: Add 1 to hit rolls for the target’s combat attacks for the rest of the turn. Then, your general’s Butcher’s Cauldron becomes **empty**.
- **Eager Procurers**（Passive）
  - 効果: While your general’s Butcher’s Cauldron is **empty**, add 2" to the Move characteristic of friendly units while they are wholly within 12" of your general.

## 強化（Mawseeker Mob Enhancements）

Give your general 1 of the following enhancements.

- **Well-stocked Larder**（Start of First Battle Round）
  - 効果: Your general’s Butcher’s Cauldron becomes **full**.
- **Disgusting Eater**（Once Per Battle, Reaction: Opponent declared a **CHARGE** ability for a visible enemy unit within 12" of this unit.）
  - 効果: Subtract D3 from the charge roll. If your general’s Butcher’s Cauldron is **full**, you can subtract 3 from the charge roll instead, but if you do so, their Butcher’s Cauldron becomes **empty** after that **CHARGE** ability has been resolved.
- **Generous Portions**（End of Any Turn）
  - 効果: If your general’s Butcher’s Cauldron is **full**, **Heal (1)** each visible friendly unit wholly within 9" of your general.
- **Hunger Pangs**（Once Per Battle, Enemy Movement Phase）
  - 効果: Each friendly unit that is not in combat can move up to D3". Friendly units that are not in combat and are wholly within 9" of your general while their Butcher’s Cauldron is **empty** can move up to D6" instead. They cannot end those moves in combat.
