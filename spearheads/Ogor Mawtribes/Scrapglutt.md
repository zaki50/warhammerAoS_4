- **It's a Hard Life**（Any Hero Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **Gnoblars** unit within your general's combat range to be the target.
  - 効果: Roll a D3. On a 2+: • **Heal (X)** your general where **X** is an amount equal to the roll. • 1 model in that **Gnoblars** unit is slain.
- **Frenzied Artillery**（Passive）
  - 効果: If the unmodified charge roll for a friendly **Gnoblar Scraplauncher** is 6+, add 3 the Attacks characteristic of the target's **Companion** weapons for the rest of the turn.

## 強化（Scrapglutt Enhancements）

Give your general 1 of the following enhancements.

- **Nasty Trap**（Once Per Battle, Your Movement Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick an enemy unit within 12" of your general to be the target.
  - 効果: Give the target your general's **Trap** token. While the target has a **Trap** token, each time the target is picked to use a **MOVE** or **CHARGE** ability, roll a dice. On a 4+, that **MOVE** or **CHARGE** ability has no effect and the target's **Trap** token is removed
- **Veteran Hunter**（Once Per Battle, Any Hero Phase.）
  - 宣言: Pick an enemy unit within 12" of your general to be the target.
  - 効果: For the rest of the turn, add 1 to the Rend characteristic of your general's combat attacks that target that unit.
- **Massive Bulk**（Passive）
  - 効果: Add 2 to the Health characteristic of your general.
- **Savage Instincts**（Reaction: You declared a FIGHT ability for your general）
  - 効果: Pick a friendly unit that has not used a **FIGHT** ability this turn and is within your general's combat range to be the target. The target can be picked to use a **FIGHT** ability immediately after the **FIGHT** ability used by your general has been resolved.
