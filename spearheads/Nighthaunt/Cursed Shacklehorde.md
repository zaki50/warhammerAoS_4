## バトル特性（Cursed Shacklehorde Battle Traits）

- **Cackling Arrival**（Once Per Turn (Army), Your Movement Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick 1 of your units in reserve.
  - 効果: Set up that unit anywhere on the battlefield more than 6" from all enemy units.
- **Ethereal**（Passive）
  - 効果: Ignore negative modifiers to save rolls for friendly units.

## レジメントアビリティ（Cursed Shacklehorde Regiment Abilities）

Pick 1 of the following regiment abilities.

- **Discorporate**（Once Per Turn (Army), Any Hero Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly unit to be the target.
  - 効果: Roll a dice. On a 3+, add 1 to save rolls for the target for the rest of the turn.
- **Mounting Dread**（Once Per Turn (Army), End of Any Turn）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly unit to be the target.
  - 効果: Roll a dice. On a 2+, for the rest of the turn, subtract the current battle round number from the control scores of enemy units while they are in combat with the target.

## 強化（Cursed Shacklehorde Enhancements）

Give your general 1 of the following enhancements.

- **Unholy Visage**（Your Hero Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a visible enemy unit within 6" of your general to be the target.
  - 効果: Roll a dice. On a 3+, the target must immediately use a **RETREAT** ability as if it were the enemy movement phase. Immediately after that ability has been resolved, if the target is in combat, inflict 2D3 mortal damage on it.
- **Deathly Possessor**（Any Combat Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick an enemy **HERO** within 12" of your general to be the target, then pick another enemy unit within the target's combat range to be the victim.
  - 効果: Roll a D3. On a 2+, pick one: • Inflict mortal damage on the target equal to the roll. • Inflict mortal damage on the victim equal to the roll.
- **Tales of Horror**（Once Per Battle, Any Combat Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick an enemy **INFANTRY** or **CAVALRY** unit within 6" of your general to be the target.
  - 効果: For the rest of the battle, if the target is replaced using the 'Call for Reinforcements' ability, halve the number of models in the replacement unit, rounding up.
- **Spectral Howl**（Once Per Battle, Any Hero Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick an enemy unit within 12" of your general to be the target.
  - 効果: For the rest of the turn, subtract 1 from the number of dice rolled when making charge rolls for the target, to a minimum of 1.
