## バトル特性（Slasher Host Battle Traits）

- **Wave of Terror**（Any Charge Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly unit to use this ability if it charged this phase and the charge roll was 10+. Then, pick an enemy unit within 1" of it to be the target.
  - 効果: The target has **STRIKE-LAST** this turn.
- **Ethereal**（Passive）
  - 効果: Ignore all modifiers to save rolls for friendly units (positive and negative).

## レジメントアビリティ（Slasher Host Regiment Abilities）

Pick 1 of the following regiment abilities.

- **Death Stalkers**（Start of the First Battle Round）
  - 宣言: Pick an enemy unit on the battlefield.
  - 効果: Add 1 to the Rend characteristic of melee weapons used for attacks that target that unit.
- **Chorus of Terror**（Passive）
  - 効果: Subtract 1 from hit rolls for combat attacks that target a friendly unit that charged in the same turn.

## 強化（Slasher Host Enhancements）

Give your general 1 of the following enhancements.

- **Shadow's Edge**（Passive）
  - 効果: Your general’s **Sword of Stolen Hours** has **Crit (Mortal)**.
- **Beacon of Nagashizzar**（Once Per Battle, Your Hero Phase）
  - 効果: Return 1 slain model to each friendly unit on the battlefield.
- **Soulfire Ring**（End of Any Turn）
  - 効果: If any models were slain by your general this turn, **Heal (D6)** your general.
- **Cloaked in Shadow**（Passive）
  - 効果: No more than 1 enemy unit can target your general with attacks (shooting or combat) per phase.
