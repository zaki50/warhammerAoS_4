## バトル特性（Warpspark Clawpack Battle Traits）

- **Always Three Clawsteps Ahead**（Once Per Phase, Enemy Movement Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly unit that is not in combat.
  - 効果: That unit can use the ‘Normal Move’ ability as if it were your movement phase.

## レジメントアビリティ（Warpspark Clawpack Regiment Abilities）

Pick 1 of the following regiment abilities.

- **Endless Swarm of Rats**（Passive）
  - 効果: When a friendly **Clanrats** unit uses its ‘Seething Swarm’ ability, you can return D6 slain models to that unit instead of D3.
- **Warpstone-laced Armour**（Once Per Battle, Reaction: Opponent declared an ATTACK ability and targeted your Stormfiends unit）
  - 効果: Your **Stormfiends** unit has **WARD (5+)** for the rest of the turn.

## 強化（Warpspark Clawpack Enhancements）

Give your general 1 of the following enhancements.

- **Cage of Warp Lightning**（Once Per Battle, Any Combat Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a visible enemy unit within 6" of your general and roll a dice.
  - 効果: On a 2+, the enemy unit has **STRIKE-LAST** this phase. On a 1, inflict 1 mortal damage on your general.
- **Scurry Away**（Any Combat Phase）
  - 効果: Roll a dice. On a 3+, this unit can immediately use the ‘Retreat’ ability as if it were your movement phase. If it does so, no mortal damage is inflicted on it.
- **Skilled Manipulator**（Passive）
  - 効果: Your general has **WARD (4+)** while they are within 1" of any friendly **Clanrats** units.
- **Skitterleap**（Your Hero Phase）
  - 宣言: Make a casting roll of 2D6.
  - 効果: On a 6+, remove your general from the battlefield and set them up again on the battlefield more than 6" from all enemy units. They cannot use **MOVE** abilities in the following movement phase.
