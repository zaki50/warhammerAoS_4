## バトル特性（Vigilant Brotherhood Battle Traits）

- **Shield of Azyr**（Once Per Turn, Your Hero Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly unit.
  - 効果: Until the start of your next turn, that unit has **WARD (5+)**.
- **Storm Charge**（Once Per Battle, Your Charge Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly unit that is not in combat.
  - 効果: That unit can use **CHARGE** abilities this turn even if it used a **RUN** ability in the same turn.

## レジメントアビリティ（Vigilant Brotherhood Regiment Abilities）

Pick 1 of the following regiment abilities.

- **Strike Where Needed**（Once Per Battle, Reaction: You declared a RETREAT ability）
  - 効果: No mortal damage is inflicted on that unit by that **RETREAT** ability. In addition, that unit can still use **CHARGE** abilities this turn even though it used a **RETREAT** ability.
- **Blaze of Glory**（Once Per Battle, Any Combat Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly unit that is in combat.
  - 効果: Until the end of the phase, each time a model in that unit is slain, make a **vengeance roll** of D6. On a 4+, inflict 1 mortal damage on an enemy unit in combat with that unit.

## 強化（Vigilant Brotherhood Enhancements）

Give your general 1 of the following enhancements.

- **Morrda's Talon**（Passive）
  - 効果: Your general’s **Hallowed Greataxe** has **Crit (Mortal)**.
- **Quicksilver Draught**（Once Per Battle, Any Combat Phase）
  - 効果: Your general has **STRIKE-FIRST** this phase.
- **Hallowed Scrolls**（Passive）
  - 効果: Your general has **WARD (5+)**.
- **Null Pendant**（Once Per Battle, End of Any Turn）
  - 宣言: Roll a dice for each enemy unit contesting the same objective as your general.
  - 効果: On a 2+, subtract the roll from the control score of that enemy unit this turn.
