## バトル特性（Yndrasta's Spearhead Battle Traits）

- **Lightning-Strike Arrival**（Your Movement Phase）
  - 効果: Set up this unit anywhere on the battlefield more than 6" from all enemy units.

## レジメントアビリティ（Yndrasta's Spearhead Regiment Abilities）

Pick 1 of the following regiment abilities.

- **Defend to the Last**（Passive）
  - 効果: Friendly units have **WARD (6+)** while they are contesting an objective you control.
- **Drive Them Back**（End of Any Turn）
  - 宣言: Pick any number of friendly units that are both contesting an objective and in combat.
  - 効果: Each of those units can make a pile-in move (Core Rules, 15.3). For each unit that did so, pick an enemy unit within 1" of it and roll a dice. On a 4+, inflict 1 mortal damage on that enemy unit.

## 強化（Yndrasta's Spearhead Enhancements）

Give your general 1 of the following enhancements.

- **Dazzling Radiance**（Once Per Battle, Your Movement Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick your general to use this ability if they were set up this phase.
  - 効果: You can return 1 slain model to each friendly unit wholly within 12" of your general.
- **The Prime Huntress**（Passive）
  - 効果: The Damage characteristic of **Thengavar** (Yndrasta’s spear) is 2D6 for attacks that target a **MONSTER**.
- **Strike with the Tempest's Rage**（Passive）
  - 効果: Your general has **STRIKE-FIRST** if they charged in the same turn.
- **Hawk of the Celestial Skies**（Once Per Battle, Any Combat Phase）
  - 効果: Until the end of the phase, add 1 to hit rolls for attacks made by friendly units while they are wholly within 12" of your general.
