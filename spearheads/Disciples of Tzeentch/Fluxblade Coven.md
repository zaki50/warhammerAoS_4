## 戦闘特性（Fluxblade Coven Battle Traits）

- **Masters of Destiny**（Once per Battle, Start of the First Battle Round）
  - 効果: Roll 9 dice and put them to one side. These are your **destiny** **dice**. During the battle, instead of rolling the dice for 1 of the rolls from the list below, you can pick one of your **destiny** **dice** and use it as the roll. Once a destiny dice has been used, it is discarded. If you want to replace a roll that uses more than one D6, you must use the same number of **destiny dice** (e.g. you would need to use 2 **destiny dice** in place of a 2D6 casting roll). Rolls that are replaced count as unmodified rolls and cannot be re-rolled or modified unless noted. The following rolls can be replaced with **destiny dice**: • Casting rolls • Run rolls • Charge rolls • Hit rolls • Wound rolls • Save rolls – you must still modify the roll by the Rend characteristic of the attacking weapon.

## 連隊アビリティ（Fluxblade Coven Regiment Abilities）

Pick 1 of the following regiment abilities.

- **Transient Forms**（Passive）
  - 効果: Roll a dice each time a friendly **Kairic Acolytes** model is slain in the combat phase. On a 4+, you can return 1 slain model to a friendly **Tzaangors** unit within 9" of the slain model.
- **Eternal Conflagration**（Passive）
  - 効果: Add 1 to the Rend characteristic of ranged weapons used by friendly **Flamers of Tzeentch** units.

## 強化（Fluxblade Coven Enhancements）

Give your general 1 of the following enhancements.

- **Daemonheart**（Once Per Battle, Any Combat Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick an enemy unit within 1" of your general.
  - 効果: Inflict an amount of **mortal damage** on that unit equal to the number of the current battle round.
- **Shield of Fate**（Your Hero Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a visible friendly unit wholly within 18" of your general, then make a casting roll of 2D6.
  - 効果: On a 6+, until the start of your next turn, that unit has **WARD (6+)**. If that unit already has a ward save, add 1 to ward rolls for that unit until the start of your next turn instead.
- **Timeslip Pendant**（Once Per Battle, Any Combat Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick an enemy unit within 9" of your general.
  - 効果: That unit has **STRIKE-LAST** this phase.
- **Glimpse the Future**（Your Hero Phase）
  - 宣言: If you have fewer than 6 **destiny dice**, make a casting roll of 2D6.
  - 効果: On a 7+, you can roll a dice and add it to your **destiny dice**.
