## 戦闘特性（Sunblooded Prowlers Battle Traits）

- **Chameleon Ambush**（Your Movement Phase）
  - 効果: Set up this unit anywhere on the battlefield more than 6" from all enemy units.
- **Vengeance of Azyr**（Once Per Turn (Army), End of Any Turn）
  - 宣言: Pick each enemy unit in combat with a friendly **SAURUS** unit to be the targets.
  - 効果: Roll a dice for each target. On a 4+, inflict 1 mortal damage on the target.

## 連隊アビリティ（Sunblooded Prowlers Regiment Abilities）

Pick 1 of the following regiment abilities.

- **Scaled Aegis**（Your Hero Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **SAURUS** unit to be the target
  - 効果: Roll a dice. On a 3+, that unit has **WARD (6+)** until the start of your next turn.
- **Followers of Huanchi**（Your Shooting Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **SKINK** unit to be the target.
  - 効果: Roll a dice. On a 3+, the target's ranged weapons have **Crit (Auto-wound)** for the rest of the turn, including **Companion** weapons.

## 強化（Sunblooded Prowlers Enhancements）

Give your general 1 of the following enhancements.

- **Instinctive Commander**（Your Hero Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a visible friendly unit wholly within 12" of your general and not in combat to be the target.
  - 効果: Roll a dice. On a 3+, the target can immediately use the 'Normal Move' ability as if it were your movement phase.
- **Venomite Swarm**（Any Combat Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick an enemy unit in combat with your general to be the target.
  - 効果: If your general's **Venomites** token is on the battlefield, roll a D3. On a 1, remove that token from the battlefield. On a 2+, inflict an amount of mortal damage on the target equal to the roll.
- **Blessed by the Old Ones**（End of Any Turn）
  - 宣言: Your general must use this ability if it has been destroyed. Roll a dice.
  - 効果: On a 2+, you can set up a replacement unit with 1 **Sunblood** model anywhere on the battlefield more than 6" from all enemy units.
- **Savage Mauling**（Any Combat Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick an enemy unit in combat with your general to be the target.
  - 効果: Ward rolls cannot be made for the target for the rest of the turn.
