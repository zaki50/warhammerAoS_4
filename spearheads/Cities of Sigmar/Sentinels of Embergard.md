## バトル特性（Sentinels of Embergard Battle Traits）

- **Ranger Doctrines**（Once Per Turn (Army), Reaction: You declared a **FIGHT** ability for your general or a friendly non-**HERO** unit wholly within 12" of your general）
  - 効果: Immediately after that **FIGHT** ability has been resolved, pick a point on the battlefield within 6" of your general. Remove the unit using this ability from the battlefield and set it up again within 1" of that point and not in combat.

## レジメントアビリティ（Sentinels of Embergard Regiment Abilities）

Pick 1 of the following regiment abilities:

- **Close-Quarters Drill**（Passive）
  - 効果: Friendly units’ ranged weapons have **Shoot in Combat** but you must subtract 1 from hit rolls for their shooting attacks while they are in combat.
- **Come and Take it, Blaggards!**（Passive）
  - 効果: Add 5 to the control scores of friendly non-**HERO** units while they are in combat with any enemy units that have 6 or more models or a Health characteristic of 3 or more.

## 強化（Sentinels of Embergard Enhancements）

Give your general 1 of the following enhancements.

- **Throwing Knives**（Any Shooting Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a visible enemy unit within 10" of your general to be the target.
  - 効果: &#x20;Roll a dice. On a 3+, inflict 1 mortal damage on the target.
- **Trained Ranger**（Once Per Battle, Your Movement Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly unit wholly within 6" of a battlefield edge to be the target.
  - 効果: Remove the target from the battlefield and set it up again wholly within 3" of a battlefield edge and more than 6" from all enemy units.
- **Hot-Blooded Endurance**（Passive）
  - 効果: Your general can use **CHARGE** abilities even if they used a **RUN** or **RETREAT** ability in the same turn and no mortal damage is inflicted on them by **RETREAT** abilities.
- **Blade of the Kraken**（Once Per Battle, Any Combat Phase）
  - 効果: Double the Attacks characteristic of your general’s melee weapons for the rest of the turn.
