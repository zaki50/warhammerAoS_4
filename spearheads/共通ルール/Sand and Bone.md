## Spearhead Sand and Bone Terrain Abilities

- **Unholy Draught**（Once Per Turn (Army), Any Combat Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly unit within 6" of a **Crypt of Blood** terrain feature to be the target.
  - 効果: Roll a dice. Add 1 to the roll if you are the **underdog.** On a 4+, add 1 to hit rolls for the target's combat attacks for the rest of the turn. Otherwise, inflict D3 mortal damage on the target.
- **Veiled Promises**（Once Per Turn (Army), End of Any Turn）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly unit within 6" of an **Icon of Nulahmia** terrain feature to be the target.
  - 効果: Roll a dice. Add 1 to the roll if you are the **underdog.** On a 4+, add 3 to the target's control score for the rest of the turn. Otherwise, subtract 3 from the target's control score for the rest of the turn.
