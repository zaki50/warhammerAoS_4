## バトル特性（Bleak Host Battle Traits）

- **Nurgle's Embrace**（End of Any Turn）
  - 効果: Spend any number of your **disease points**. For each disease point you spend, pick an enemy unit that is in combat with any of your units and roll a dice. On a 5+, inflict 1 mortal damage on that unit (you can pick the same unit more than once).
- **Daemonic Summoning**（Your Movement Phase）
  - 効果: Set up this unit anywhere on the battlefield more than 6" from all enemy units.
- **Diseased**（Passive）
  - 効果: Each time an attack made by a friendly model scores a critical hit, you receive 1 **disease point**, to a maximum of 7.

## レジメントアビリティ（Bleak Host Regiment Abilities）

Pick 1 of the following regiment abilities.

- **Infested with Wonders**（Passive）
  - 効果: Each time a friendly model is slain, before it is removed from play, you can pick an enemy unit within 1" of it and roll a dice. On a 4+, inflict 1 mortal damage on that unit.
- **Locus of Fecundity**（Once Per Phase, Your Hero Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly unit.
  - 効果: **Heal (D3)** that unit.

## 強化（Bleak Host Enhancements）

Give your general 1 of the following enhancements.

- **Summoner of Plaguebearers**（End of Any Turn）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **Plaguebearers** unit wholly within 14" of your general.
  - 効果: Return 1 slain model to that unit.
- **Gift of Febrile Frenzy**（Once Per Battle, Any Combat Phase）
  - 効果: Until the end of the phase, add 1 to the Attacks characteristic of melee weapons used by friendly units while they are wholly within 7" of your general.
- **Gardener of Nurgle**（Your Movement Phase）
  - 宣言: If your general is contesting an objective not contested by any enemy models, roll a dice.
  - 効果: On a 3+, that objective is considered by you to be **desecrated**. Friendly units have **WARD (4+)** while they are contesting a desecrated objective. If your opponent gains control of a desecrated objective, it is no longer desecrated.
- **Pestilent Breath**（Your Shooting Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick an enemy unit within 7" of your general and roll a dice for each model in that unit.
  - 効果: For each 5+, inflict 1 mortal damage on that unit.
