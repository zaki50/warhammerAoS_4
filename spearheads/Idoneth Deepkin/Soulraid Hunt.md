## 強化（Soulraid Hunt Enhancements）

Give your general 1 of the following enhancements.

- **Steelshell Armour**（Passive）
  - 効果: Ignore all modifiers to save rolls for your general (positive and negative).
- **Arch-Ritualist**（Passive）
  - 効果: Add 1 to rolls for your general’s ‘Ritual of the Creeping Mist’ ability.
- **Mind Flare**（Once Per Battle, Any Combat Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick an enemy unit in combat with your general.
  - 効果: Until the end of the phase, attacks made by that unit only score hits on unmodified hit rolls of 6.
- **Delicious Morsels**（Your Hero Phase）
  - 効果: **Heal (1)** each friendly **CAVALRY** unit within your general’s combat range.
