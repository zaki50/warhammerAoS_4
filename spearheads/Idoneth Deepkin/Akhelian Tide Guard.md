- **The Spear of Asphoren**（Once Per Turn (Army), Any Charge Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly unit that is not in combat to use this ability.
  - 効果: Add 1 to charge rolls for that unit for the rest of the turn.
- **The Shield of Ulchiss**（Once Per Turn (Army), Your Movement Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly unit in combat to use this ability.
  - 効果: For the rest of the turn, each time that unit uses a **RETREAT** ability, no mortal damage is inflicted on it.

## 強化（Akhelian Tide Guard）

Give your general 1 of the following enhancements.

- **Dutiful Souls**（Once Per Battle, Your Hero Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly unit wholly within 12" of your general to be the target.
  - 効果: You can return 1 slain model to the target unit. If the target is an **INFANTRY** unit, you can return D3 slain models to it instead.
- **Shimmering Amulet**（Passive）
  - 効果: Your general has **WARD (5+)**.
- **Voltaic Charge**（Passive）
  - 効果: Your general’s **Akhelian Royal Weapons** have **Charge (+1 Damage)**.
- **Soul Stealer**（End of Any Turn）
  - 効果: If your general is in combat, **Heal (D3)** your general.
