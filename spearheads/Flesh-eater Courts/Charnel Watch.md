## バトル特性（Charel Guard Battle Traits）

- **Delusions and Madness**（Once Per Battle Round (Army), Start of Battle Round）
  - 宣言: You must use this ability at the start of each battle round. If it is the first battle round, pick a **DELUSION**. Otherwise, make a delusion roll by rolling a D6.
  - 効果: On a 1-3, you must pick a different **DELUSION** to the one you picked last time. On a 4+, you must pick the same **DELUSION** as last time. For the rest of the battle round, you believe the **DELUSION** you picked.
- **Delusion of the Great Feast**（Once Per Turn (Army), End of Any Turn）
  - 宣言: If you believe this **DELUSION**, pick each friendly unit on the battlefield to be the targets.
  - 効果: **Heal (1)** each target. If the target is a **SERFS** unit, return D3 slain models to it instead.
- **Delusion of the Knightly Host**（Passive）
  - 効果: While you believe this **DELUSION**, if the unmodified charge roll for a friendly **HERO** or **KNIGHTS** unit is 8+, add 1 to hit rolls for that unit’s combat attacks for the rest of the turn.

## レジメントアビリティ（Charnel Throne Regiment Abilities）

Pick 1 of the following regiment abilities.

- **Delusion of the Sentinel**（Passive）
  - 効果: While you believe this **DELUSION**, add 1 to ward rolls for friendly units while each model in the unit is contesting an objective.
- **Delusion of the Hunter**（Passive）
  - 効果: While you believe this **DELUSION**, add 1 to wound rolls for combat attacks made by friendly units while no models in the unit are contesting an objective.

## 強化（Charnel Watch Enhancements）

Give your general 1 of the following enhancements.

- **Almost Lucid**（Once Per Battle, Reaction: You declared the ‘Delusions and Madness’ ability）
  - 効果: You can re-roll the delusion roll.
- **Companion of the Hunt**（End of Any Turn）
  - 効果: If your general is not in combat, they can move 3". They cannot move into combat during any part of that move.
- **A Worthy Challenge**（Once Per Battle, Any Combat Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick an enemy unit that started the battle with 3 or fewer models and is in combat with your general to be the target.
  - 効果: Your opponent must decide whether the target will accept or refuse your general’s challenge. If they **accept**, for the rest of the phase, when your general and the target are picked to use a **FIGHT** ability, all of their attacks must target each other. If they **refuse**, the target has **STRIKE-LAST** for the rest of the phase.
- **Choirmaster**（Once Per Battle, Your Shooting Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a visible enemy unit within 6" of your general to be the target.
  - 効果: For the rest of the turn, add 1 to the Damage characteristic of friendly units’ ranged weapons for attacks that target that enemy unit.
