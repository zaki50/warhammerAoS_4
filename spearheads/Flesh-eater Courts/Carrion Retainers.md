## バトル特性（Carrion Retainers Battle Traits）

- **Feeding Frenzy**（Passive）
  - 効果: Add 1 to the Attacks characteristic of melee weapons used by friendly units while they are wholly within 12" of any friendly **HEROES** that have 6 **noble deeds points**.
- **Noble Deeds**（Passive）
  - 効果: Each time a friendly **HERO** uses a **FIGHT** ability, after its attacks have been resolved, give that **HERO** a number of **noble deeds points** equal to the number of damage points allocated by that ability. Each **HERO** can have a maximum of 6 **noble deeds points** at any time.
- **Summon Loyal Subjects**（Your Movement Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **HERO** with any **noble deeds points** to use this ability.
  - 効果: Spend any number of that **HERO**’s **noble deeds points** as follows: Pick a friendly **Cryptguard** unit within 9" of this unit and spend 1 **noble deeds point** to return 1 model to that unit. Pick a friendly **Morbheg Knights** unit within 9" of this unit and spend 2 **noble deeds points** to return 1 model to that unit.

## レジメントアビリティ（Carrion Retainers Regiment Abiities）

Pick 1 of the following regiment abilities.

- **Defenders of the Realm**（Passive）
  - 効果: Add 1 to save rolls for friendly units that are contesting an objective you control.
- **Crusading Army**（Passive）
  - 効果: Add 1 to run rolls and charge rolls for friendly units.

## 強化（Carrion Retainers Enhancements）

Give your general 1 of the following enhancements.

- **Blood-river Chalice**（Once Per Battle, Your Hero Phase）
  - 効果: **Heal (2D3)** your general.
- **Rousing Oration**（Your Hero Phase）
  - 効果: Roll a dice for each friendly unit wholly within 12" of your general. Do not roll a dice for your general. For each 5+, give 1 **noble deeds point** to your general.
- **Crimson Victuals**（Your Hero Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a visible enemy unit within 18" of your general to be the target, then make a casting roll of 2D6.
  - 効果: On a 6+, inflict D3 mortal damage on the target. Then, if your **Cryptguard** unit is within 6" of the target, you can return 1 slain model to your **Cryptguard** unit for each damage point allocated by this ability.
- **Ulguan Cloak**（Passive）
  - 効果: Your general is not visible to enemy models that are more than 12" away from them.
