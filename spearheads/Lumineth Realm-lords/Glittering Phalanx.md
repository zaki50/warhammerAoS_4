## バトル特性（Glittering Phalanx Battle Traits）

- **Lightning Reactions**（Passive）
  - 効果: When players are alternating picking units to use a **FIGHT** ability, when it is your turn to pick a unit, you can pick 2 units instead of 1. Resolve the second **FIGHT** ability immediately after the first.
- **Power of Hysh**（Once Per Turn (Army), Your Hero Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly unit.
  - 効果: Roll a dice. On a 2+, until the start of your next turn, attacks made by that unit score critical hits on unmodified hit rolls of 5+.
- **Shining Company**（Passive）
  - 効果: Subtract 1 from hit rolls that target friendly units.
- **Facets of War**（Once Per Battle Round, Start of Battle Round）
  - 効果: You must use this ability at the start of the battle round. Pick 1 **FACET OF WAR** ability. That **FACET OF WAR** ability can be used this battle round but the other cannot.

## レジメントアビリティ（Glittering Phalanx Regiment Abilities）

Pick 1 of the following regiment abilities.

- **Heightened Reflexes**（Passive）
  - 効果: Add 1 to save rolls for friendly units that use a **FIGHT** ability immediately after another friendly unit because of the ‘Lightning Reactions’ ability, until the end of the phase.
- **Arcane Prowess**（Passive）
  - 効果: Add 1 to casting rolls for your general.

## 強化（Glittering Phalanx Enhancements）

Give your general 1 of the following enhancements.

- **Waystone**（Your Movement Phase）
  - 効果: Remove your general from the battlefield and set them up again anywhere on the battlefield more than 6" from all enemy units.
- **Protection of Hysh**（Your Hero Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a visible friendly unit wholly within 12" of your general to be the target, then make a casting roll of 2D6.
  - 効果: On a 7+, the target has **WARD (5+)** until the start of your next turn.
- **Speed of Hysh**（Your Hero Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a visible friendly unit wholly within 18" of your general, then make a casting roll of 2D6.
  - 効果: On a 5+, double the Move characteristic of that unit until the start of your next turn.
- **Overwhelming Heat**（Your Hero Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a visible enemy unit within 24" of your general, then make a casting roll of 2D6.
  - 効果: On a 7+, halve the Move characteristic of that unit until the start of your next turn and roll a dice. If the roll equals or exceeds that unit’s Save characteristic, inflict D3 mortal damage on it.
