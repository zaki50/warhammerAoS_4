## バトル特性（Heartflayer Troupe Battle Traits）


## レジメントアビリティ（Heartflayer Troupe Regiment Abilities）

Pick 1 of the following regiment abilities.

- **Murderous Epiphany**（Once Per Battle, Your Hero Phase）
  - 効果: All friendly units gain the **Blood Rites** passive ability they would have gained at the start of the next battle round (they keep this ability for the rest of the battle, but they do not gain it for a second time at the start of the next battle round).
- **Blessing of Khaine**（Any Combat Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly unit wholly within 12" of your general. You cannot pick your general.
  - 効果: Add 1 to ward rolls for that unit this phase.

## 強化（Heartflayer Troupe Enhancements）

Give your general 1 of the following enhancements.

- **Flask of Shademist**（Once Per Battle, Any Combat Phase）
  - 効果: Until the end of the phase, subtract 1 from hit rolls for attacks that target friendly units while they are wholly within 12" of your general.
- **Fuelled by Revenge**（Passive）
  - 効果: Add 1 to the Rend characteristic of melee weapons used by friendly **Blood Stalkers** units while they are wholly within 12" of your general.
- **Zealous Orator**（Your Hero Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly unit wholly within 9" of your general that is **not** in combat. Roll a dice for each slain model from that unit.
  - 効果: For each 5+, you can return 1 slain model to that unit.
- **Bathed in Blood**（Passive）
  - 効果: Each time a model is slain by your general, **Heal (1)** your general.
