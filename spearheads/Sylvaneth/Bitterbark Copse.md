## 戦闘特性（Bitterbark Copse Battle Traits）

- **Strike and Fade**（Once Per Turn, End of Any Turn）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly unit that used a **FIGHT** ability this turn and is within 3" of any terrain features, then roll a dice.
  - 効果: On a 2+, remove that unit from the battlefield and set it up again so that each model in the unit is within 3" of any terrain features and more than 6" from all enemy units.
- **Ley Lines**（Once Per Turn, End of Any Turn）
  - 効果: **Heal (1)** each friendly unit that is within 3" of any terrain features.

## 連隊アビリティ（Bitterbark Copse Regiment Abilities）

Pick 1 of the following regiment abilities.

- **Walkers of the Hidden Paths**（Once Per Turn, Your Movement Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly unit that is within 3" of any terrain features and not in combat.
  - 効果: Remove that unit from the battlefield and set it up again more than 6" from all enemy units. That unit cannot use **MOVE** abilities for the rest of the phase.
- **Vengeful Spirits of the Land**（Once Per Turn, End of Any Turn）
  - 宣言: Pick an enemy unit that is contesting an objective and roll a dice.
  - 効果: On a 4+, inflict D3 mortal damage on that enemy unit.

## 強化（Bitterbark Copse Enhancements）

Give your general 1 of the following enhancements.

- **Treesong**（Your Hero Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a visible friendly unit wholly within 12" of your general to be the target, then make a casting roll of 2D6.
  - 効果: On a 7+, add 1 to the Rend characteristic of the target unit’s melee weapons until the start of your next turn.
- **Gnarled Warrior**（Passive）
  - 効果: Ignore negative modifiers to save rolls for your general.
- **Regrowth**（Your Hero Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a visible friendly unit wholly within 18" of your general to be the target, then make a casting roll of 2D6.
  - 効果: On a 5+, **Heal (D6)** the target.
- **Seed of Rebirth**（Passive）
  - 効果: If your general would be destroyed, before removing them from play, roll a dice. On a 3+, your general is not destroyed and any remaining damage points inflicted on them have no effect. Then, **Heal (1)** your general. This unit cannot use this ability again for the rest of the battle.
