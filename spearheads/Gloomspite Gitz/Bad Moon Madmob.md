## バトル特性（Bad Moon Madmob Battle Traits）

- **Squigalanche**（Your Movement Phase）
  - 効果: Set up this unit wholly within your territory, wholly within 3" of a battlefield edge and more than 6" from all enemy units.
- **Under the Light of the Bad Moon**（Once Per Battle, Start of the First Battle Round）
  - 宣言: Pick a territory (either friendly territory or enemy territory) to be **under the Light of the Bad Moon**. If both players can use this ability, the players roll off and the winner picks a territory to be under the Light of the Bad Moon. The territory picked remains under the Light of the Bad Moon in the first and second battle rounds. In the third and fourth battle rounds, the other territory is under the Light of the Bad Moon.
  - 効果: While a friendly unit is wholly within the territory that is **under the Light of the Bad Moon**, the appropriate effect below applies to it: ***Frothing Zealots:*** If the unit is a **Moonclan Stabbas** unit, add 3 to its control score. ***Lunar Squigs:*** If the unit is a **Squig Hoppers** unit, no mortal damage is inflicted on it when it uses **Retreat** abilities. ***Moonlit Hide:*** If the unit is a **Rockgut Troggoths** unit, add 1 to save rolls for it.

## レジメントアビリティ（Bad Moon Madmob Regiment Abilities）

Pick 1 of the following regiment abilities.

- **The Hand of Gork**（Once Per Battle, (Army) Your Movement Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly unit that is not in combat to use this ability.
  - 効果: Remove that unit from the battlefield and set it up again more than 9" from all enemy units.
- **The Lunatic Hordes**（Your Hero Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **Moonclan Stabbas** unit to use this ability.
  - 効果: You can return up to D3 slain models to that unit.

## 強化（Bad Moon Madmob Enhancements）

Give your general 1 of the following enhancements.

- **Hallucinogenic Fungus Brew**（Passive）
  - 効果: Your general has **WARD (4+)** in the first battle round, **WARD (5+)** in the second battle round, and **WARD (6+)** in the third and fourth battle rounds.
- **The Clammy Cowl**（Passive）
  - 効果: Subtract 1 from hit rolls for attacks that target your general.
- **Fight Another Day**（End of Any Turn）
  - 宣言: Pick your general to use this ability if they used a **FIGHT** ability this turn.
  - 効果: Your general can make a 2D6" move but cannot end that move in combat.
- **Nightshade Mushroom**（Once Per Battle, Enemy Movement Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly unit within your general’s combat range.
  - 効果: That unit cannot be targeted by shooting attacks in the next shooting phase.
