## バトル特性（Snarlpack Huntaz Battle Traits）

- **Fast As Frazzlegit**（Passive）
  - 効果: Friendly units can use **CHARGE** abilities even if they used a **RETREAT** ability in the same turn. In addition, no mortal damage is inflicted on friendly units by **RETREAT** abilities.

## レジメントアビリティ（Snarlpack Huntaz Regiment Abilities）

Pick 1 of the following regiment abilities.

- **Flankin' Force**（Once Per Battle, Deployment Phase）
  - 効果: Pick up to 2 friendly units. Remove them from the battlefield and set them up again anywhere on the battlefield more than 6" from all enemy units.
- **Frazzleboom**（Passive）
  - 効果: Each time a friendly **WAR MACHINE** is destroyed, before removing it from play, pick an enemy unit within 3" of that **WAR MACHINE** and roll a D3. On a 2+, inflict an amount of mortal damage on that unit equal to the roll.

## 強化（Snarlpack Huntaz Enhancements）

Give your general 1 of the following enhancements.

- **Sunsteala Shard**（Once Per Battle, Enemy Movement Phase）
  - 効果: For the rest of the turn, friendly units cannot be targeted by shooting attacks while they are wholly within 6" of this unit, unless the attacking unit is in combat with the target unit.
- **Glare of Frazzlegit**（Once Per Battle, Any Combat Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick an enemy unit in combat with your general to be the target.
  - 効果: Subtract 1 from hit rolls for attacks made by the target for the rest of the turn.
- **Kunnin' As A Snarlfang**（Once Per Battle, Any Combat Phase）
  - 効果: For the rest of the turn, add 1 to the Rend characteristic of melee weapons used by friendly units while they are wholly within 12" of the bearer.
- **Devious Backstabba**（End of Any Turn）
  - 宣言: Pick an enemy **HERO** in combat with this unit to be the target.
  - 効果: Roll a D3. On a 2+, inflict an amount of mortal damage on the target equal to the roll.
