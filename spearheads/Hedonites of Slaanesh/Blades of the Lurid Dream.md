## 連隊アビリティ（Blades of the Lurid Dream Regiment Abilities）

Pick 1 of the following regiment abilities.

- **Unparalleled Speed**（Once Per Battle, Any Combat Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly unit to use this ability.
  - 効果: That unit has **STRIKE-FIRST** this phase.
- **Locus of Diversion**（Your Movement Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly unit to use this ability.
  - 効果: For the rest of the turn, that unit can use a **RETREAT** ability and still use **SHOOT** and/or **CHARGE** abilities later in the turn.

## 強化（Blades of the Lurid Dream Enhancements）

Give your general 1 of the following enhancements.

- **Pendant of Slaanesh**（Your Hero Phase）
  - 効果: **Heal (D3)** your general.
- **Cacophonic Choir**（Your Hero Phase）
  - 宣言: Make a casting roll of 2D6.
  - 効果: On a 6+, make a **cacophony roll** of D6. Inflict D3 mortal damage on each enemy unit (roll for each) within 6" of your general that has a Control characteristic less than the cacophony roll.
- **Twisted Mirror**（Once Per Turn, Your Shooting Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick an enemy unit within 9" of your general and roll a dice.
  - 効果: On a 4+, subtract 1 from save rolls for that unit until the start of your next turn.
- **Sceptre of Domination**（Any Combat Phase）
  - 宣言: Roll a dice for each enemy unit in combat with your general.
  - 効果: On a 5+, the unit being rolled for has **STRIKE‑LAST** this phase.
