## バトル特性（Epicurean Revellers Battle Traits）

- **Favour Most Fickle**（Passive）
  - 効果: The following effects apply based on the number of friendly units on the battlefield. These effects are not cumulative:&#x20; **• 5 or more units:** Add 2 to the Control characteristic of friendly units. **• 4 units:** Add 1 to hit rolls and wound rolls for friendly units’ combat attacks, including attacks made with **Companion** weapons. **• 3 units:** Add 1 to run rolls and charge rolls for friendly units. **• 2 units:** Friendly units have **WARD (5+)**. **• 1 unit:** Add 2 to the Attacks characteristic of friendly units’ melee weapons, including **Companion** weapons.

## レジメントアビリティ（Epicurean Revellers Regiment Abilities）

Pick 1 of the following regiment abilities.

- **Bringers of Degradation**（Passive）
  - 効果: When players are alternating picking units to use a **FIGHT** ability, when it is your turn to pick a unit, you can pick 2 friendly **Daemonettes** units instead of 1 unit. Resolve the second **FIGHT** ability immediately after the first.
- **Daemonic Onslaught**（Once Per Battle, Deployment Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly **Seekers** or **Fiends** unit wholly within 12" of your general to be the target.
  - 効果: The target can use the ‘Normal Move’ ability as if it were your movement phase.

## 強化（Epicurean Revellers Enhancements）

Give your general 1 of the following enhancements

- **Twisted Grace**（Passive）
  - 効果: When your general makes a pile-in move, if they charged in the same turn, add D6" to the distance they can move.
- **Excess of Violence**（Once Per Battle, Any Combat Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a visible friendly unit wholly within 12" of your general to be the target.
  - 効果: The target’s melee weapons, including **Companion** weapons, have **Crit (2 Hits)** until the start of your next turn.
- **High Courtiers**（Passive）
  - 効果: While your general is wholly within the combat range of a friendly **Daemonettes** unit, both units have **WARD (5+)**.
- **Irresistible Soul-Musk**（Once Per Battle, Your Movement Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a visible friendly **Seekers** or **Fiends** unit wholly within 12" of your general to be the target.
  - 効果: Remove the target from the battlefield and set it up again on the battlefield more than 6" from all enemy units.
