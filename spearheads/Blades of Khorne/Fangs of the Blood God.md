## バトル特性（Fangs of the Blood God Battle Traits）

- **The Quarry**（Start of Battle Round）
  - 効果: If no enemy units are the **quarry**, pick an enemy unit to be the **quarry** (you can pick an enemy unit in reserve).
- **Blood-drenched**（Once Per Turn, End of Any Turn）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly unit that slew any enemy models using a **FIGHT** ability this turn to be the target.
  - 効果: For the rest of the battle, the target's melee weapons have **Crit (Mortal).**

## レジメントアビリティ（Fangs of the Blood God Regiment Abilities）

Pick 1 of the following regiment abilities.

- **The Scent of Blood**（Passive）
  - 効果: Add 1 to hit rolls and add 1 to wound rolls for combat attacks that target an enemy unit that had any damage points allocated to it this turn.
- **Savagery Upon Savagery**（Once Per Battle, Any Combat Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a friendly unit in combat to be the target.
  - 効果: For the rest of the turn, add 1 to the Attacks characteristic of the target's melee weapons. If the target is a **HERO,** add D3 to the Attacks characteristic of its melee weapons instead.

## 強化（Fangs of the Blood God Enhancements）

Give your general 1 of the following enhancements.

- **Sustained by Gore**（End of Any Turn）
  - 効果: **Heal (D3)** your general.
- **Killing Pounce**（Once Per Battle, Any Charge Phase）
  - 効果: For the rest of the turn, when making charge rolls for your general, you can roll an additional dice, to a maximum of 3, but if you do, they must finish that charge in combat with the **quarry.**
- **Evasive Hunter**（Passive）
  - 効果: Subtract 1 from hit rolls and wound rolls for shooting attacks that target your general.
- **Furious Bites**（End of Any Turn）
  - 効果: If your general is in combat with the **quarry,** inflict D3 mortal damage on the **quarry.**
