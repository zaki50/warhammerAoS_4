## バトル特性（Bloodbound Gore Pilgrims Battle Traits）

- **Heads Must Roll**（Once Per Turn, Any Hero Phase）
  - 宣言: Spend 3 **blood tithe points** and pick up to 3 friendly units.
  - 効果: Add 1 to the Rend characteristic of those units’ melee weapons until the start of your next turn.
- **The Blood Tithe**（Passive）
  - 効果: Each time a unit is destroyed during the battle, you receive 1 **blood tithe point**.
- **Murderlust**（Once Per Turn, Any Hero Phase）
  - 宣言: Spend 1 **blood tithe point** and pick up to D3 friendly units.
  - 効果: Each of those units can move D6" (roll for each).

## レジメントアビリティ（Bloodbound Gore Pilgrims Regiment Abilities）

Pick 1 of the following regiment abilities.

- **Blood-woken Runes**（Passive）
  - 効果: Friendly units have **WARD (5+)** if they have used a **FIGHT** ability in the same phase.
- **Favoured of Khorne**（Once Per Turn, Start of Your Turn）
  - 効果: Roll a dice. On a 2+, you receive 1 **blood tithe point**.

## 強化（Bloodbound Gore Pilgrims Enhancements）

Give your general 1 of the following enhancements.

- **The Crimson Plate**（Passive）
  - 効果: Your general has **WARD (5+)**.
- **Unholy Flames**（Your Hero Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a visible friendly unit wholly within 16" of your general, then make a chanting roll of D6.
  - 効果: On a 4+, add 1 to the Rend characteristic of that unit’s melee weapons until the start of your next turn.
- **Resanguination**（Your Hero Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick a visible friendly unit wholly within 16" of your general, then make a chanting roll of D6.
  - 効果: On a 3+, **Heal (D3)** that unit.
- **Headhunter**（Any Combat Phase）
  - 宣言: Pick an enemy **HERO** in combat with your general.
  - 効果: Your general has **STRIKE-FIRST** this phase, but all attacks made by them this phase must target that enemy **HERO**.
